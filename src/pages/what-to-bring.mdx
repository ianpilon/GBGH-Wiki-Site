# What to Bring

**Summary**: Bring health card + second ID + insurance proof, all medications (with a current list, dose, and instructions), personal toiletries, your own pyjamas, dressing gown, non-slip slippers. Since January 2024 GBGH has used a bring-your-own-device model for entertainment and phone calls; loaner tablet/phone available. Leave valuables and large amounts of cash at home. Electrical appliances need approval. Strict no-scent, no-latex-balloons, no-lily policy.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-what-to-bring.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Essentials

- Valid health card.
- A second piece of identification.
- Proof of any other hospital insurance.

## Medications

- Bring ALL medications you are taking: inhalers, eye drops, topical patches, creams, injectables, over-the-counter meds, herbal products.
- Bring a current list with dose and instructions.
- A medication-list form is available: `/gbghdocs/GBGH-Your-Medication-List.pdf`.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-what-to-bring.md`)

## Personal items

- Toiletries (toothbrush, toothpaste, kleenex, brushes, denture cups, shampoo, conditioner, nail clipper, bar soap, razor).
- Own pyjamas / nightgowns.
- Dressing gown.
- Non-slip slippers (a must for comfort and safety).
- Optional: reading materials, crafts, hobbies. For children: favourite book or toy.

## Devices and entertainment (since January 2024)

- Hospital transitioned to a bring-your-own-device model.
- TVs are no longer in patient rooms on 2 North and 2 East inpatient units.
- Landline phones removed from all patient rooms.
- Hospital provides free Wi-Fi.
- If you don't have a phone or tablet, hospital can lend a tablet (iPad) or phone — ask your care team.

See [Internet Phone Tv](/internet-phone-tv) for details.

## What NOT to bring

- Electrical appliances (hair dryers, electric razors) without approval by maintenance staff.
- Jewellery, wallets, purses, valuables, large amounts of cash. Leave with family at home.
- Items left in your room are at your own risk.

## Allergy and scent policy

- **No perfumes or scents**: scent-free environment to protect patients and staff with allergies and asthma.
- **No latex balloons**: only foil balloons permitted (latex allergies).
- **No strongly scented flowers**: no lilies, hyacinth, etc.
