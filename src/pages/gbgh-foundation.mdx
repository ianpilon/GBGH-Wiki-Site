# GBGH Foundation

**Summary**: The GBGH Foundation is a separate fundraising organization that supports the hospital. It is referenced from gbgh.on.ca but operates at its own domain (gbghf.ca). The Foundation has a board appointee seat on the hospital Board of Directors (currently Glenn Keenan, 2025-2026).

**Sources**: `pages/board-of-directors.md` (Glenn Keenan listed as "GBGH Foundation Board Appointee"), all GBGH page footers link to `gbghf.ca`, news posts referencing Foundation activity, the predecessor Huronia District Hospital Foundation in `pages/our-history.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What we know from scraped GBGH content

- The Foundation's site is `http://gbghf.ca` (linked from every GBGH page footer).
- The Foundation has its own executive director. News posts in the corpus reference Foundation leadership changes (e.g. "GBGH Foundation welcomes new interim leader", "GBGH Foundation welcomes new executive director").
- The Foundation has a designated seat on the hospital's Board of Directors (the GBGH Foundation Board Appointee).
- The earliest predecessor was the Huronia District Hospital Foundation (founded 1988), which raised more than $2M in its first campaign. (source: `pages/our-history.md`)
- Multiple news posts reference Foundation-mediated donations: e.g. "Port Severn couple pledges $150,000 to Georgian Bay General Hospital".

## Not in this wiki's corpus

The Foundation's own website (`gbghf.ca`) was not scraped. To build out a proper Foundation page, that site would need to be ingested as a separate raw source.
