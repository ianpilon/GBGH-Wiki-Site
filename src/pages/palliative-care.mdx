# Palliative Care and MAiD

**Summary**: GBGH launched a Medical Assistance in Dying (MAiD) and Palliative Care service for admitted patients on August 8, 2023. Five physicians with palliative expertise provide consults via a weekly on-call schedule. Care is multidisciplinary, partnered with the North Simcoe Muskoka Hospice Palliative Care Network (NSMHPCN) for continuity into the community.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-palliative-care.md`, news post "GBGH adds enhanced MAiD and palliative care to meet patients' end of life needs"

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Service launch

The MAiD and Palliative Care service launched on August 8, 2023 for admitted patients, at the request of the primary physician providing general care during the stay.

## Coverage and consult model

- Five physicians with special palliative expertise.
- Weekly on-call schedule provides comprehensive year-round coverage.
- Palliative consults result in plans/recommendations sent to the in-hospital primary physician and to community providers, supporting seamless transitions.
- MAiD consults involve eligibility assessments by the on-call palliative physician.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-palliative-care.md`)

## What palliative care is (per the hospital's framing)

Care for individuals living with a life-threatening illness when the focus has shifted from cure to comfort. Pain and symptom management are paramount. Quality of remaining life takes priority over extension of life.

## Team

Led first by the individual and family. May include physician, nurse, personal support worker, hospital chaplain or clergy, social worker, dietitian, physiotherapist, occupational therapist, respiratory therapist, speech-language therapist, recreational therapist, hospice volunteers.

## NSMHPCN partnership

The North Simcoe Muskoka Hospice Palliative Care Network places Palliative Nurse Consultants in the hospital. Combined 10+ years palliative experience, specialty preparation through education, clinical practice, and national certification. Available for in-hospital consults and post-discharge follow-up.
