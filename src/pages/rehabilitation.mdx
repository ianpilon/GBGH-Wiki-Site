# Rehabilitation Services

**Summary**: An inpatient rehab program on the first floor at the Midland site. Multidisciplinary team (PT, OT, SLP, social work, dietitian, recreation therapy, pharmacy, nursing, physician). Daily therapy Mon-Fri. Discharge planned in advance with home equipment and follow-up therapy arrangements coordinated.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-rehabilitation-services.md`, `pages/programs-and-services-rehabilitation-services-inpatient-referral-policy.md` (referral details in Phase 2 second pass)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Program approach

- **Active**: patient is expected to participate fully; family encouraged with patient's permission.
- **Coordinated**: individualized program built by the rehab team (assessment, treatment, education).
- **Independence-oriented**: goal is daily activities of living with minimal assistance.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-rehabilitation-services.md`)

## Team

Possible team members: physician, nurse, occupational therapist (OT), physiotherapist (PT), pharmacist, recreation therapist, speech-language pathologist (SLP), social worker, dietitian, discharge planner, home and community care coordinator, chaplain, rehabilitation assistants. The patient is the most important team member.

## Schedule and expectations

- Individual and group therapy sessions Mon-Fri excluding holidays.
- Patients may be asked to do additional exercises in off-hours.
- Expected to take an active role in daily activities and personal care.
- Expected to attend the dining room for lunch and dinner.
- Family may take patients on passes, assist with therapy, attend meetings (with patient consent).

## What to bring

Clothing (undergarments, socks, jogging suit or comfortable slacks, shorts, shirts/blouses, sleepwear, housecoat, slippers, supportive rubber-soled shoes), and personal articles (toiletries, hearing aids/batteries, glasses, razor, cosmetics, facial tissue, mobility aids if owned).

## Discharge

- Length of stay depends on treatment plan; discharge date communicated in advance.
- Equipment needs assessed; rental or purchase options explained.
- Therapists discuss post-discharge therapy options; arranging those appointments is the patient's responsibility.
- Discharge package: follow-up instructions, home therapy programs, prescriptions.
- Patient responsible to arrange a ride by 11am on the morning of discharge.
- Patient expected to follow up with family doctor right after discharge.

## Other rules

- Identity labels on personal items.
- Laundry: patient or family responsibility; laundry facilities are available on the unit.
- Leave valuables at home; small cash for incidentals can be kept in a safe.
- No smoking on hospital property.
