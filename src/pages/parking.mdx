# Parking

**Summary**: GBGH charges hourly and pass-based parking fees. First 30 minutes free, $4/hour, $10/day, $35/week, $80/month. The hospital is required to raise 16% of its annual operating budget from sources like parking. Despite this, parking ran net losses in both 2023-24 and 2024-25 after expenses and depreciation.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-parking.md`, `pdfs/GBGH_ParkingPolicy_January2025.pdf` (not yet extracted), news posts ("GBGH waives parking fees in recognition of difficult times", "GBGH resumes paid public parking")

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Why parking is paid

Hospitals are responsible for all costs related to parking facilities: construction, equipment, maintenance. The Ministry of Health provides no support, and the Broader Public Sector Parking Directive caps the ability to increase visitor parking revenue. Per the hospital, fees collected go to patient care via the annual operating budget. GBGH must raise 16% of operating budget annually.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-parking.md`)

## Rates

| Duration | Cost |
|---|---|
| 30 minutes or less | Free |
| Hourly | $4.00 |
| Single day pass (up to 24 hours, no in/out) | $10.00 |
| Weekly pass (in/out) | $35.00 |
| Monthly pass (in/out) | $80.00 |

Longer passes give better rates. Keep ticket with you (not in vehicle) and pay at machines in the main lobby or ED entrance before leaving.

## Emergency drop-off

There is an emergency drop-off area but it must be clear for ambulances. After dropping off a patient, the driver must move to the parking lot ($4/hour up to $10/24 hours).

## Net revenue (last two reported years)

| | 2023/24 | 2024/25 |
|---|---|---|
| Gross parking revenue | $806,324.71 | $762,454.00 |
| Total parking expenses | ($407,096.76) | ($540,721.00) |
| Total depreciation / loss on disposal | ($137,908.94) | ($123,631.00) |
| **Net revenue** | **($269,187.82)** | **($98,102.00)** |

Both years: parking lost money for the hospital after all costs.
