# Patient Education Handouts

**Summary**: Index of GBGH's procedure-specific patient education handouts. 35 handout PDFs are in the scraped corpus, covering surgical procedures across general, ENT, ophthalmology, urology, gynecology, orthopedics, and a general post-operative guide. All are downloadable from the [Surgical Services](/surgical-services) page on gbgh.on.ca and stored locally in `raw/pdfs/`.

**Sources**: All `pdfs/Patient-Handout-*.pdf`, plus a few without that prefix (Adenoids, Myringotomy, Nasal-Surgery, Tonsillectomy, Guide-for-Surgery-2025-ENG)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Notes

- All handouts are PDFs as scraped from the [Surgical Services](/surgical-services) page.
- Several have duplicated versions (e.g. pre-anesthetic questionnaire has three: `-ENG`, `-ENG-1`, `-ENG-4`). The most recent is likely `-ENG-4`.
- Two ENT handouts (Myringotomy, Nasal Surgery) carry "patient-handbook-dec-2025" in the filename, suggesting they were updated in December 2025.
- Patient-facing forms (not handouts but related): Mammography Requisition, Ultrasound Requisition, X-Ray Requisition, CT Requisition, MRI Request Requisition, PFT Requisition.
