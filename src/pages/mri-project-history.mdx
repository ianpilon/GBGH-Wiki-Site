# MRI Project History

**Summary**: GBGH's path to its first MRI took roughly two years from funding announcement to operational. Funded February 2023, ground broken June 2024, MRI delivered January 2025, officially opened March 21, 2025. Construction also included a new mammography space (announced October 2025).

## Why it mattered

Before the MRI install, North Simcoe patients needing MRI scans had to travel out of the area. The hospital framed this as "bringing care closer to home" across the entire project's public messaging.

## What followed

Construction of a new mammography space at GBGH was announced October 28, 2025. Mammography Phase 1 (screening) launched April 13, 2026; diagnostic on May 4, 2026. See [Mammography](/mammography).

Subsequent funding support for advanced imaging at GBGH was announced via the County of Simcoe (August 2024). See post `county-of-simcoe-supports-local-access-to-advanced-imaging-at-gbgh`.
