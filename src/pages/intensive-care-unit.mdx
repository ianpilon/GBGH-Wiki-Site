# Intensive Care Unit

**Summary**: GBGH's ICU is a Level 3 unit (one of the highest service levels in Ontario), with six critical-care beds plus telemetry monitoring of 10 inpatient beds. Staffed by internists, registered nurses, respiratory therapists, and other specialties as needed. Located on the main floor of the Midland site.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-emergency-department-intensive-care-unit.md`, news post "GBGH ICU achieves Level 3 Basic designation"

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Designation

Level 3 ICU. The hospital states this is "one of the highest levels of service in the province of Ontario" for patients needing advanced or ongoing respiratory and life support.

A news post in the corpus, "GBGH ICU achieves Level 3 Basic designation", marks the moment this designation was earned. Date to be confirmed in [Timeline](/timeline).

## Capacity

- 6 critical-care beds.
- 10 medical inpatient beds monitored via the ICU's telemetry system, in collaboration with the inpatient unit. Lets the ICU support a wider patient population than its bed count alone.

## Team

- Internists (specialist physicians).
- Registered nurses.
- Respiratory therapists.
- Other specialties as required.
