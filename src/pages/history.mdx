# History

**Summary**: Timeline of GBGH and its two predecessor hospitals. The Midland site dates to 1905. The Penetanguishene site was founded in 1911 and run by the Grey Sisters of the Immaculate Conception from 1942 until it closed in February 2022. The two hospitals integrated formally as Georgian Bay General Hospital in 2008.

**Sources**: `pages/our-history.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Midland site timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1905 | Midland/Penetanguishene Marine and General Hospital opens in Sunnyside. 12 beds, $0.70/day per patient. |
| 1906 | Hospital auxiliary formed by community women. |
| 1911 | Penetanguishene builds its own hospital. Midland hospital name shortens. |
| 1918 | Hospital moves to the Manley Chew home on Bay Street (gift from James Playfair). Renamed St. Andrew's Hospital. |
| 1976 | Huronia District Hospital opens (replaces St. Andrew's). |
| Late 1980s | Planning begins for expansion of emergency, obstetrics, and laboratory services. |
| 1988 | Huronia District Hospital Foundation founded; capital campaign raises >$2M. |

## Penetanguishene site timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1610 | French voyageurs arrive in Huronia. Jesuit missionaries later open first hospital in Ontario at Ste. Marie Among the Hurons. |
| Early 1900s | Diphtheria epidemic prompts demand for hospital services. |
| 1911-04-14 | The Penetanguishene Hospital incorporated. |
| 1912–1929 | School of Nursing operates. |
| 1942-08-15 | Six Grey Sisters of the Immaculate Conception arrive to take over operations at the request of Monsignor Jean Castex. |
| 1946 | Planning begins for a new hospital facility. |
| 1954-07-25 | New hospital opens. |
| 1963 | Grey Sisters purchase the hospital from the town. |
| 1973 | Emergency, outpatient, and physiotherapy unit added. |
| 1981 | Chronic/rehabilitation unit added. |
| 1992-09 | Approved as a regional rehabilitation centre (15 beds reclassified). |
| 2022-02 | Penetanguishene site closes. |

## Integration timeline

| Year | Event |
|---|---|
| 1992-10 | Two hospitals form the North Simcoe Hospital Alliance (voluntary). |
| 1996 | Service-transfer plan implemented. Integrated management team and common medical staff formed. |
| 2007 | Attempt at formal integration under Catholic Health Association of Ontario sponsorship fails. |
| 2008 | Provincial supervisor recommends integration. Two sites integrated into one secular organization. The neighbouring psychiatric hospital is divested from the province to the CHAO-sponsored Penetanguishene General Hospital Inc. |
| 2009-12 | Community-wide vote selects "Georgian Bay General Hospital" name. Vision announced: "Exceptional Care to every Person, Every Time." |
