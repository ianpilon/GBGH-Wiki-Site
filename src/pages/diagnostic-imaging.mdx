# Diagnostic Imaging

**Summary**: The Diagnostic Imaging department at the Midland site covers X-ray, ultrasound, CT, echocardiography (satellite of RVH), MRI (operational 2025), and mammography (Phase 1 screening from April 13, 2026; diagnostic from May 4, 2026). All exams require a physician's order and a valid health card.

## General logistics

- Located on the main floor of the Midland site, just down the hall from registration.
- Arrive early; registration uses a centralized numbering system in the main lobby.
- All exams require a physician's order. Health card must be valid (not expired).
- For non-MRI/non-mammography services: Monday-Friday 8am-4pm. Emergency services available outside those hours.

## Echocardiography

Satellite space hosted in partnership with RVH (Royal Victoria Regional Health Centre, Barrie). Echocardiogram is an ultrasound of the heart producing 2D and 3D images of heart structure and function. Used to diagnose heart murmurs, valve function, cardiac masses, hypertension, and heart disease. Referral form: `pdfs/Echocardiography-Requisition-GBGH.pdf`.

## Records and images for outside use

Patients can request copies of their images to take to another facility. Must request 24 hours in advance. Picked up at the Diagnostic Imaging reception desk during business hours with valid ID.
