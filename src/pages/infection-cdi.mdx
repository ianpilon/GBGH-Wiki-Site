# C. difficile Infection (CDI) Rate

**Summary**: GBGH reported 2 CDI cases in Q1 FY 25/26 (rate 0.19), 4 cases in Q2 (rate 0.37, a 50% increase), and 2 cases in Q3 (rate 0.18). The Q2 spike was followed by a return to the Q1 level.

**Sources**: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Clostridium-Difficile-Infection-CDI-Rate.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Numbers (FY 25/26)

| Quarter | Cases | Quarterly rate |
|---|---|---|
| Q1 | 2 | 0.19 |
| Q2 | 4 | 0.37 |
| Q3 | 2 | 0.18 |

The rate is per 1,000 patient days (standard CDI reporting). Q2 saw a 50% jump in case count and roughly doubled the rate; Q3 returned to baseline.

(source: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Clostridium-Difficile-Infection-CDI-Rate.pdf`)

## Why C. difficile is tracked

Clostridium difficile is one of the antibiotic-resistant pathogens the hospital's IPAC program explicitly targets. It is named on the [Patient Safety](/patient-safety) page alongside MRSA and VRE.
