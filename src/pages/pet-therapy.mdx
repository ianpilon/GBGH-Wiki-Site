# Pet Visitation and Pet Therapy

**Summary**: GBGH has introduced a Pet Visitation and Pet Therapy program for dogs and cats over one year old with vaccinations and calm temperaments. As of the most recent page update, Pet Visitation Applications are paused. Service animals are always permitted without advance approval.

**Sources**: `pages/pet-visitation-and-pet-therapy.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Pet Visitation criteria

Allowed:

- Dogs or cats.
- Over one year of age.
- All required vaccinations.
- Calm temperament: no barking, nipping, jumping, or uncontrollable energy.
- On a short leash no longer than 2 metres (not retractable).

Other animal species require approval by the Volunteer and Student Coordinator and the Unit Manager. Approved only in extremely limited circumstances.

(source: `pages/pet-visitation-and-pet-therapy.md`)

## When pets cannot visit

No pet may visit within one week of:

- Vomiting or diarrhea.
- Urinary or fecal incontinence.
- Sneezing or coughing of unknown origin.
- Open wounds.
- Ear infections.
- Skin infections.
- Ticks, fleas, or other infestations.

## Process

- Application required.
- Approval by a supervisor or manager.
- GBGH Approved Pet ID Tag issued; must be worn on each visit.
- **Pet Visitation Applications are currently paused** (per the page; updates will be shared when applications reopen).

## Service animals

- Do **not** require advance approval.
- Patient should notify their care team in advance.
- Documentation may be requested and must be produced.
- All service animals must wear identification.
- Documentation of the service animal's role should be in the patient chart.
