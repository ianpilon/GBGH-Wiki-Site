# Patient Relations

**Summary**: The Patient Relations Coordinator handles patient concerns, compliments, and complaints. First step: speak directly with your nurse, team leader, manager, or attending physician. For urgent issues or unresolved concerns, contact Patient Relations at 705-526-1300 ext. 5641. Initial update on a concern within 72 hours. Raising a concern does not affect care received.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-relations.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Process

1. Speak with the nurse, team leader, manager, or attending physician first.
2. If concern is urgent or unresolved, contact Patient Relations:
   - Phone: 705-526-1300 ext. 5641 or 705-797-3130.
   - Email: `PatientRelations@gbgh.on.ca`.
   - Mail: Patient Relations, Georgian Bay General Hospital, P.O. Box 760, Midland, ON L4R 4P4.
3. Update on the initial concern within 72 hours.

Office hours: Monday-Friday 8am-4pm (excluding statutory holidays).

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-relations.md`)

## What Patient Relations does

- Listens to concerns.
- Communicates issues to the appropriate care team member, ensures timely response.
- Receives compliments and shares with the healthcare team and administration.
- Advocates and escalates when required.
- Acts as ongoing point of contact through resolution.

Resolution path may involve a Senior Team member, the full care team, or the Chief of Staff/Service depending on the concern.

## Anonymity

Anonymous contact is allowed, but the hospital is limited in how it can respond without your contact information.

## Will care be affected?

Per the hospital page: no. Concerns are viewed as a learning and improvement opportunity. Raising a concern will not have a negative effect on care received at GBGH.

## Privacy

To address concerns, the hospital needs information about the patient and the care or service in question. Information is protected under PHIPA (see [Health Records And Privacy](/health-records-and-privacy)). The hospital works directly with the patient or, with consent, with someone authorized by the patient.
