# No Smoking and Vaping

**Summary**: GBGH has been fully smoke and vape free across all sites and grounds since Canada Health Day, May 12, 2007. The policy covers parking lots, walkways, and vehicles. Patients needing hospital equipment (IV monitors, etc.) cannot leave the property to smoke. Non-compliance can result in fines under the Smoke Free Ontario Act, eviction of visitors, or discharge/transfer of patients.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-no-smoking.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Policy in effect since 2007-05-12

Both GBGH sites became fully smoke and vape free on Canada Health Day, May 12, 2007. The policy applies to:

- All patients, visitors, and staff.
- All hospital grounds and buildings.
- Walkways.
- Vehicles in parking lots.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-no-smoking.md`)

## Rationale (per the hospital)

- Protects patients, staff, and visitors.
- Motivates people, including staff, to quit or reduce smoking.
- Signals prevention is as important as treatment for illness and disease.

## To smoke or vape

- Must leave hospital property.
- Hospital staff cannot escort patients off-property; arrangements are the patient's or family's responsibility.
- Patients connected to hospital equipment (IV monitors, etc.) must not leave the property.

## Consequences of non-compliance

- Fines per the Smoke Free Ontario Act and municipal by-laws.
- Eviction of visitors.
- Discharge or transfer of patients.
