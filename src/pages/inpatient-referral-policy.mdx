# Inpatient Rehab Referral Policy

**Summary**: Inpatient rehab referrals to the GBGH Midland site require three PDF forms (referral, patient consent, discharge/transfer checklist) faxed to 705-526-0069. Significant changes to a patient's status (medical, functional, infection, equipment) require notifying the team leader at 705-526-1300 ext. 5611.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-rehabilitation-services-inpatient-referral-policy.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## How to refer

Print and complete three PDFs, then fax to GBGH Midland site at **705-526-0069**:

1. Inpatient Rehab Referral Form: `/gbghdocs/GBGH-Rehab-Inpatient-Referral-Form.pdf`
2. Patient Consent Form: `/gbghdocs/GBGH-Rehab-Referral-Patient-Consent.pdf`
3. Discharge/Transfer Checklist: `/gbghdocs/GBGH-Rehab-Discharge-Transfer-Checklist.pdf`

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-rehabilitation-services-inpatient-referral-policy.md`)

Note: these forms are under `/gbghdocs/` (a different path from the `/gbghmedia/` paths captured in the PDF scrape), so they are not in this wiki's PDF corpus.

## Patient status changes

If there is any significant change or deterioration in the patient's status (medical, functional, infection, or equipment needs), notify the team leader at GBGH Midland: **705-526-1300 ext. 5611**.

## Discharge/Transfer Checklist

On patient transfer, the referrer must refer to the Discharge/Transfer Checklist regarding the information that must accompany the patient.
