# Emergency Department

**Summary**: The Midland-site ED provides 24-hour emergency service to North Simcoe. Average ~120 patients per day, with volumes sometimes doubling in summer due to seasonal residents and tourism. ~39,700 annual visits in 2021-2022. Supported by internal medicine, general surgery, ophthalmology, dentistry, obstetrics, and gynecology. Critical transfers go through CritiCall.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-emergency-department.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Volume and scope

- 24/7 service for North Simcoe County residents and visitors.
- ~120 patients/day average; doubles in summer.
- 2021-2022 annual visits: ~39,700.
- Backed by internal medicine, general surgery, ophthalmology, dentistry, obstetrics, gynecology.
- Patients needing other specialties are transferred to other facilities via CritiCall (the provincial transfer service that finds an accepting hospital and arranges transport).

## Alternatives the hospital actively promotes

GBGH explicitly lists alternatives to the ED on its own page to reduce non-urgent visits:

- Family physician or nurse practitioner.
- Pharmacist (now able to prescribe for minor ailments: pink eye, UTIs, dermatitis, allergies, acid reflux, parasitic worms; also prescription renewals for blood pressure, diabetes, asthma).
- Virtual Urgent Care Clinic: regional-virtual-urgent-care.ca (same-day / next-day with a Nurse Practitioner).
- Health811: free 24/7 nurse phone line at 811 or chat at ontario.ca/health811.
- Mental Health Crisis Services: CMHA Simcoe County 1-888-893-8333; Suicide Crisis Line 988; Wendat self-referral crisis appointments.
- North Simcoe Family Health Team Nurse Practitioners: 705-526-7804 ext. 0 (non-urgent minor illnesses).
- Pediatric Rapid Access Care Clinic: care-clinics.ca (children 6 months to 18 years, non-life-threatening).
- HIV/STI self-test kits via GetaKit (free, mailed to home, 17+).
- Rocket Doctor virtual same-day appointments: rocketdoctor.ca/georgian-bay-general-hospital/ (used as an ED alternative; GBGH partners with them).

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-emergency-department.md`)

## Wait times

A live wait-times page is hosted at gbgh-predict.oculys.com (linked via [Ed Wait Times Clock](/ed-wait-times-clock)). The hospital cautions wait times can change without warning and are not guaranteed. Patients are seen by severity, not arrival order.

## What to bring to the ED

- Health card and a piece of photo ID with current address.
- Reading material; minimize accompanying visitors to reduce waiting-room congestion.
- Note: hospital does not have a public pharmacy for dispensing ED prescriptions.

## When to come

The hospital page lists these as appropriate ED reasons:

- Change in mental status (confusion).
- Mental health crisis.
- Seizures or convulsions.
- Broken bone or wound.
- Chest pain or tightness.
- Baby under 3 months with fever.
- Severe vomiting or diarrhea.
- Stiff neck and light sensitivity.
- Severe headache and dizziness.
- Difficulty breathing.
