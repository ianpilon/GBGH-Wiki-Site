# ED Wait Times Clock

**Summary**: A live wait-times page for the GBGH Emergency Department, hosted by Oculys at gbgh-predict.oculys.com. The hospital surfaces both the live clock and the Rocket Doctor partnership on this page, framing them together as alternatives to ED visits.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-emergency-department-wait-times-clock.md`, news post "GBGH launches Emergency Wait Times clock webpage"

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it is

A live ED wait-times display at https://gbgh-predict.oculys.com/ (provider: Oculys). The hospital's page wraps the link with a Terms of Use agreement and a strong disclaimer that:

- Wait times can change significantly and immediately, without warning.
- Wait times are not guaranteed.
- Patients are seen based on medical condition severity, not arrival order or displayed wait time.
- Wait-time information is not medical advice and not a recommendation to choose a different ED.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-emergency-department-wait-times-clock.md`)

## Rocket Doctor (cross-link on this page)

The wait-times page also surfaces the Rocket Doctor partnership as an ED alternative. Rocket Doctor is recommended specifically for patients without a family doctor.

- Partnership URL: https://rocketdoctor.ca/georgian-bay-general-hospital/
- A dedicated news post explains the trial: "GBGH exploring innovative ways to bring appropriate care to the community with Rocket Doctor."
