# Mammography Program

**Summary**: Mammography is GBGH's newest imaging modality. Phase 1 (screening for asymptomatic patients) began April 13, 2026 to fit the CAR-MAP accreditation process. Diagnostic (symptomatic) requisitions accepted starting May 4, 2026. Phase 2 will add self-referrals. Most exams take 15-20 minutes. Construction news posts indicate a dedicated mammography space was built.

## Rollout

- **Phase 1** (effective 2026-04-13): screening mammography for asymptomatic patients. Cases selected to meet the CAR-MAP accreditation process (CAR-MAP is the Canadian Association of Radiologists Mammography Accreditation Program).
- **Diagnostic (symptomatic)** requisitions: from 2026-05-04.
- **Phase 2**: self-referrals will be accepted once launched.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging.md` and `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-mammography-program.md`)

## What mammography is

Low-dose X-ray of the breast used to look for signs of breast cancer. Two types:

- **Screening mammography**: checks for cancer before symptoms appear; both breasts; helps catch small lumps and tissue changes before they're palpable.
- **Diagnostic mammography**: done when there's a concern (lump, pain, nipple discharge, skin changes); more detailed and multi-angle.

## Where and who performs it

- Diagnostic Imaging, main level near the Emergency Department.
- Medical Radiation Technologist (MRT) performs the exam; a Radiologist with specialty qualifications reviews and interprets.

## Duration

- 15-20 minutes typical.
- Diagnostic may run longer if extra images are needed.

## Preparation

- Inform provider if pregnant or possibly pregnant (may recommend timing or starting with ultrasound).
- If breastfeeding: pump or nurse just before to empty breasts; improves comfort and image quality; do not need to discard pumped milk afterward.
- If still menstruating: avoid the week before and during menstruation; breasts may be tender.
- Inform scheduler about breast implants or recent vaccination.
- Day of: normal routine (eat, drink, take medications).
- Avoid deodorant, perfume, lotion, body powder (affect image quality).
- Undress from the waist up; gown provided; zip-up or button-down shirts are convenient.

## During the exam

- Stand in front of the mammogram machine.
- One breast at a time positioned on a support plate.
- Plastic plate compresses the breast for a few seconds while images are taken.
- May feel pressure or mild discomfort; tell technologist if uncomfortable.
- Process repeated to ensure all breast tissue is imaged.

## After

- Resume normal activities immediately.
- Results sent to ordering physician; also available on the MyHealth Portal (see [My Health Care](/my-health-care)).
- If follow-up imaging is needed, Diagnostic Imaging staff contact the patient directly.
