# Patient and Visitor Index

**Summary**: Index of practical patient and visitor information at GBGH. Covers directions, parking, visiting, what to bring, on-site services, internet/devices, no-smoking, pets, programs supporting patients (Indigenous Patient Navigator, Essential Care Partner, PFAC), patient safety, patient relations, health records and privacy, and ways for community members to support patients.

**Sources**: All `pages/patient-and-visitor-*.md` files plus `pages/pet-visitation-and-pet-therapy.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Getting here and parking

- [Directions](/directions) — driving from Highway 400 / 93.
- [Parking](/parking) — $4/hr; $80/month pass; first 30 min free; net loss for the hospital both 2023-24 and 2024-25.

## During a visit

- [Visiting Hours](/visiting-hours) — 24/7 family presence since May 24, 2023; ED entrance required for late-night arrivals; masking required.
- [On Site Services](/on-site-services) — ATM, Gift Shop, Captain's Café (volunteer-run), Café hours.
- [Internet Phone Tv](/internet-phone-tv) — free Wi-Fi; **bring your own device** model since January 2024 (TVs removed from 2 North/2 East; landlines removed from all patient rooms; hospital can lend a tablet or phone).
- [No Smoking](/no-smoking) — fully smoke and vape free since May 12, 2007; off-property only.
- [Pet Therapy](/pet-therapy) — Pet Visitation program (currently paused); service animals always permitted.

## What to bring

- [What To Bring](/what-to-bring) — health card + ID, all medications, toiletries, own pyjamas; no electrical appliances; no scents, latex balloons, or strongly scented flowers.

## Programs that support patients

- [Essential Care Partner](/essential-care-partner) — designated companion for vulnerable patients with a ≥3 day stay; not a visitor; eligible units: 1 North, 2 North, 2 East, ICU.
- [Indigenous Patient Navigator](/indigenous-patient-navigator) — supports FNMI patients; access to Traditional Healing and Smudge Room (Neegan Naabwag).
- [Patient Family Advisory Council](/patient-family-advisory-council) — PFAC seeks community members to advise on hospital experience.

## Safety, complaints, records

- [Patient Safety](/patient-safety) — five Ontario Hospital Association patient safety tips; hand hygiene; IPAC.
- [Patient Relations](/patient-relations) — formal feedback / complaints process; 705-526-1300 ext. 5641.
- [Health Records And Privacy](/health-records-and-privacy) — Privacy (PHIPA + FIPPA); requesting health records; Regional Privacy Office; Ambient AI Scribe (AI-assisted note-taking in trial).
- [Spiritual Support](/spiritual-support) — 24/7 on-call chaplain via nurse; quiet room on lower level.

## Letting the hospital know

- [Thinking Of You](/thinking-of-you) — email loved ones at ThinkingOfYou@gbgh.on.ca; messages printed and delivered weekday mornings.
- [Thank Your Care Team](/thank-your-care-team) — web form to send compliments to specific staff.
