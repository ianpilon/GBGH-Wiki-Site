# Ambulatory Care

**Summary**: Outpatient clinic providing primary and secondary care to both outpatients and inpatients in North Simcoe. Located on the lower level at the Midland site. Integrated team includes physicians, RNs, RPNs, pharmacy, lab, diagnostic imaging, and physiotherapy.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-ambulatory-care.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Scope

Provides primary and secondary care to North Simcoe County residents (outpatients and inpatients). The integrated team includes physicians, registered nurses, registered practical nurses, pharmacy, laboratory, diagnostic imaging, and physiotherapy. Supported by registration, volunteers, central supply, laundry, housekeeping, and dietary services.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-ambulatory-care.md`)

## Logistics

- Patients with Ambulatory Care appointments are asked to arrive 30 minutes early to register.
- Registration is centralized in the main lobby.
- Ambulatory Care is on the lower level at the Midland site, accessible via stairs from the main lobby or the elevator.

## Connection to other services

The Ambulatory Care waiting room is where Endoscopy and Colonoscopy patients meet their nurses post-procedure (see [Surgical Services](/surgical-services)).

The hospital's Ambulatory Care page also references the [Dialysis](/dialysis) satellite at 611 Prospect Boulevard, but that is a separate site, not part of Ambulatory Care proper.
