# Patient Safety

**Summary**: GBGH frames patient safety as a shared responsibility (patients, families, staff, physicians). The page lists five Ontario Hospital Association patient-safety tips, emphasizes hand hygiene (Just Clean Your Hands program), and outlines an active Infection Prevention and Control program targeting C. difficile, MRSA, and VRE. Surveyors during the 2023 accreditation specifically praised the medication safety framework and falls prevention program.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-safety.md`, `pages/accreditation-awards.md` (for accreditation-specific safety strengths)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Five OHA patient-safety tips (cited on the page)

1. Be involved in your health care; speak up about questions or concerns.
2. Tell the care team about past illnesses and current health conditions.
3. Bring all your medications when you go to the hospital or any appointment.
4. Tell the care team if you have ever had an allergic or bad reaction to any medication or food.
5. Make sure you know what to do when you go home from the hospital or appointment.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-safety.md`)

## Just Clean Your Hands

GBGH participates in the Ministry of Health's "Just Clean Your Hands" initiative. The hospital asks patients and visitors to clean hands on entry, throughout the visit (hand-cleaning stations are placed throughout the hospital), and on exit. Hand hygiene compliance is publicly reported quarterly: see [Hand Hygiene](/hand-hygiene) (Phase 4).

## Infection Prevention and Control (IPAC)

Targeted antibiotic-resistant infections: C. difficile, MRSA, VRE.

Aggressive procedures listed:

- Heightened surveillance by frontline staff and physicians, coordinated by a dedicated infection control team.
- Placement of hand hygiene stations throughout the hospital and in patient areas.
- Increased frequency and intensity of cleaning in all patient care areas.
- Dedicated medical equipment, when possible, for patients colonized with an antibiotic resistant bacterium.
- Corporate-wide hand hygiene program.
- Increased education for staff, physicians, patients, and families.

Public reporting on infection rates: see [Public Reporting](/public-reporting) and Phase 4 pages [Infection Cdi](/infection-cdi), [Infection Mrsa](/infection-mrsa), [Infection Vre](/infection-vre), [Infection Cli](/infection-cli), [Infection Vap](/infection-vap).

## Visiting policy as a safety tool

Visitors are asked to postpone visits when feeling unwell. See [Visiting Hours](/visiting-hours) for masking and self-screening.

## Strengths noted by surveyors (2023 accreditation)

- Safety rounds and quality plans (standardized approach).
- Medication safety framework.
- Falls prevention program.
