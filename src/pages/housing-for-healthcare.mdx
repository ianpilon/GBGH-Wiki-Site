# Housing for Healthcare

**Summary**: An online housing marketplace launched January 3, 2024 that connects community property owners with prospective and current GBGH staff. Built by GBGH's Human Resources team as a recruitment tool when rental housing emerges as a deciding factor for candidates. As of the scrape (May 2026), there are 41 property listings in the system.

**Sources**: `pages/gbgh-launches-housing-for-healthcare-to-connect-property-owners-with-prospective-hospital-staff-seeking-accommodation.md`, all 41 entries under `pages/programs-and-services-...` (actually under `/property/`), `pages/properties.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Why it exists

Per the launch announcement (January 3, 2024), local rental-market conditions were a recruitment barrier. GBGH HR built the site to remove that obstacle. Quoted in the launch:

> "If rental housing is a barrier to highly-skilled staff selecting our hospital for their career, then we want to do what we can to remove that obstacle."  
> — Angela Wiggins, VP People & Culture and CHRO, GBGH

(source: `pages/gbgh-launches-housing-for-healthcare-to-connect-property-owners-with-prospective-hospital-staff-seeking-accommodation.md`)

## How it works

- Property owners list rentals with info and photos.
- HR directs prospective hires to the resource when housing is a recruitment factor.
- Candidates can browse listings on their own at `gbgh.on.ca/housing-for-healthcare/`.

The launch announcement gives one example: a property owner near the hospital (5-minute walk) who provides a fully furnished suite for locum physicians.

## Scale

41 property listings appear in the GBGH "Properties Archive" as of the May 2026 scrape. Listings include addresses in:

- Midland (St., Ave, Blvd locations).
- Surrounding townships (e.g. Tiny Beaches Road South, Beck Boulevard, Mundy's Bay Road).
- Some duplicates appear (e.g. 92 Balm Beach Rd W and 486 Hannah St each listed twice).

The full list of addresses is in `raw/_index.json` under entries with `/property/` URLs. They are not summarized individually here because each listing is just an address with rental info, not knowledge-worthy on its own.

## Strategic context

This program is a tangible expression of the [Strategic Plan](/strategic-plan)'s "Sustainable Growth" theme: "Address recruitment in a way that accents our positive and supportive culture." Also relevant to the "Dedicated Team" theme.
