# Discharge Planning

**Summary**: Discharge time is 10am. Discharge planners coordinate with patients, family, physicians, other providers, and community services to determine post-discharge needs. Before leaving, patients visit the cashier/switchboard to pay any uncovered charges (telephone, TV rental, ambulance) and pick up valuables. Out-of-country patients must pay all outstanding charges at discharge.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-discharge-planning.md`, `pages/patient-and-visitor-discharge-planning.md` (likely duplicate content; check in Phase 3)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Process

- **Discharge time**: 10am.
- Discharge planning professionals work with patient, family, physician, other care providers, and community health service providers to determine the support and assistance needed post-discharge.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-discharge-planning.md`)

## Before leaving the hospital

- Visit cashier or switchboard to pay charges not covered by insurance: telephone, TV rental, ambulance, etc.
- Pick up any valuables previously left for safekeeping.
- Out-of-country patients: all outstanding charges must be paid at the time of discharge.

## Connection to other programs

Discharge planning is also a touchpoint with:

- [Gbgh At Home](/gbgh-at-home) (post-discharge in-home support, up to 16 weeks).
- [Rehabilitation](/rehabilitation) and [Complex Continuing Care](/complex-continuing-care) (with their own discharge processes).
- Home and Community Care Support Services (Ontario Health) for ongoing care.
