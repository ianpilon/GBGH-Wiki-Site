# Food Services

**Summary**: Food Services handles patient meals (one-week menu, mostly scratch-made, bedside selection) and runs the cafeteria on the lower level. A Registered Dietitian is on staff for clinical nutrition assessment. Cafeteria is open Mon-Fri (closed weekends and holidays); cash, debit, and credit accepted.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-dietary-services.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Patient meals

- One-week patient menu; most items made from scratch in-house.
- Food Services staff take meal selections at the bedside.
- Dietary requirements and allergies are recorded in specialized dietary software.
- For patients who cannot have their order taken, appropriate meal selections are made.
- A Registered Dietitian is on staff for clinical nutrition assessment or intervention.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-dietary-services.md`)

## Cafeteria

- Lower level of the Midland site.
- Selection: sandwiches, salads, hot entrees, snacks, beverages.
- Payment: cash, debit, credit.

### Hours (Mon-Fri, closed Sat/Sun and statutory holidays)

| Time | Service |
|---|---|
| 8:30 am | Open: coffee, muffins, snacks |
| 9:00-10:30 am | Hot breakfast |
| 10:30-11:30 am | Closed |
| 11:30 am-1:30 pm | Hot lunch |
| 1:30 pm | Closed for the day |
