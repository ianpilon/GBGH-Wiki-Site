# Spiritual Support

**Summary**: GBGH commits to respecting patients' rights to have spiritual/religious needs met, or to refuse unwanted religious visits. A 24-hour on-call Chaplain Service is available via the nurse on duty, drawing on Huronia-area clergy, lay pastoral visitors from parishes, and volunteer chaplains. A Spiritual Care Quiet Room is open to anyone on the lower level.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-spiritual-support.md`, `pages/patient-and-visitor-privacy-statement.md` (religious-affiliation disclosure)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Patient rights

Per the hospital page, patients have the right to:

- Have their religious or spiritual needs met.
- Refuse unwanted religious visits.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-spiritual-support.md`)

## 24/7 chaplain service

A 24-hour on-call Chaplain Service is available. Composition:

- Clergy of the Huronia area.
- Lay pastoral visitors representing their parishes (and/or providing volunteer pastoral care).
- Volunteer on-call chaplains.

**How to access**: ask the nurse on duty.

## Spiritual Care Quiet Room

- Location: lower level.
- Open to everyone.

## Privacy note: religious-affiliation disclosure

Per the [Health Records And Privacy](/health-records-and-privacy) page: unless you tell the hospital otherwise, GBGH may give your name, hospital location, and religious affiliation to spiritual-support representatives, but **only if you have stated a religious affiliation**.
