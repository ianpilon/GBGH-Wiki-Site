# GBGH Overview

**Official hospital website:** [gbgh.on.ca](https://gbgh.on.ca)

**Summary**: Georgian Bay General Hospital (GBGH) is a community hospital in Midland, Ontario serving North Simcoe County. Formed in 2008 from the integration of Huronia District Hospital (Midland) and Penetanguishene General Hospital. The Penetanguishene site closed in February 2022, leaving a single Midland site.

**Sources**: `pages/_home.md`, `pages/our-history.md`, `pages/contact-us.md`, `pages/accreditation-awards.md`, `pages/board-of-directors.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What GBGH is

A secular community hospital, the result of a 16-year integration process between two predecessor hospitals (1992 alliance, 2008 formal integration, 2009 naming). Vision: "Exceptional Care to every Person, Every Time" (source: `pages/our-history.md`).

Currently operates from one site:

- 1112 St. Andrew's Drive, Midland, ON L4R 4P4
- Phone: 705-526-1300

(source: `pages/contact-us.md`)

## Predecessor hospitals

- **Huronia District Hospital** (Midland), opened 1976. Roots back to 1905 (Midland/Penetanguishene Marine and General Hospital, 12 beds).
- **Penetanguishene General Hospital**, incorporated 1911. Operated 1942–2017 under the Grey Sisters of the Immaculate Conception. Closed February 2022.

See [History](/history) for full timeline.

## Quality status

Accredited with Exemplary Standing by Accreditation Canada (November 2023 survey). Met >99% of ~2,100 standards and 100% of the 30 Required Organizational Practices. Last surveyed in 2019 (98% / 100%). See [Accreditation And Awards](/accreditation-and-awards).

## Current strategic direction

Strategic Plan 2023–2030 organized around four themes: Continual Excellence, Trust & Belonging, Dedicated Team, Sustainable Growth. See [Strategic Plan](/strategic-plan).

## Notable recent investments

- New MRI installed and opened 2024–2025 (first MRI for the hospital).
- Mammography space construction (2025).
- ED redevelopment (multi-phase, completed).
- New dialysis site in Midland.
