# Obstetrical Unit

**Summary**: GBGH operates a Level I obstetrical unit at the Midland site (family physicians, midwives, general surgeons, nurses). Higher-risk deliveries are referred to Orillia Soldiers' Memorial Hospital (Level II). The GBGH Prenatal Clinic closed effective December 15, 2025; prenatal care has transitioned to community providers, but birthing services continue on site.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-obstetrical-unit.md`, multiple news posts on the birthing program

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Level of care

- Level I obstetrical care.
- Higher-risk deliveries and those needing a paediatrician or obstetrician are partnered with Orillia Soldiers' Memorial Hospital (Level II, paediatricians and obstetricians on staff). Referred to as the "Joint Birthing Program."

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-obstetrical-unit.md`)

## Care providers

Family physicians, midwives, general surgeons, registered nurses.

## During-stay policies

- Two support people may accompany the mother during labour.
- Baby rooms in with the mother during the hospital stay.
- Visiting hours: 9am-8pm; partners can stay outside those hours; siblings welcome but must be supervised.
- Vaginal delivery post-partum stay is ~24 hours. Cesarean stay is ~48 hours.
- Mom and baby get matching numbered bracelets; the hospital asks that babies not be left unattended and that all staff handling them have visible identification.

## What to bring (summary)

Labour and delivery: watch/timer, lip protection, pen, lotion, two pairs of socks, housecoat, kleenex, hair elastic.

After delivery: toiletries, change of clothes, breast pads, slippers, blow dryer, water bottle, snacks, larger bra (1 cup up), nightgowns, sanitary napkins (no tampons).

Baby items: newborn diapers, heavy baby blanket, wipes/cloths, vaseline or coconut oil, comb, car seat (close to discharge).
