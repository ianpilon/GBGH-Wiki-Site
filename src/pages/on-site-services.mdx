# On-Site Services

**Summary**: GBGH lobby amenities include ATMs, a volunteer-operated Gift Shop, the main Café on the first level, and the volunteer-operated Captain's Café in the lobby (proceeds to the GBGH Foundation). Captain's Café hours are longer than the main Café.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-on-site-services.md`, `pages/programs-and-services-dietary-services.md` (Café details)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Gift Shop

- Volunteer-operated, open daily.
- Located in the hospital lobby.
- **Exclusive supplier of GBGH brand merchandise**: ball caps, T-shirts, sweatshirts, teddy bears for newborns.
- Also offers GBGH spirit wear, snacks, gift items, flowers, personal-care essentials.

## Captain's Café (lobby, volunteer-operated)

- Located in the lobby.
- Open 8:00 am - 4:00 pm.
- Run by GBGH volunteers.
- Proceeds donated to the [Gbgh Foundation](/people/gbgh-foundation) to support the hospital.
