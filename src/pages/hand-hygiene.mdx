# Hand Hygiene Compliance

**Summary**: GBGH's hand hygiene compliance has declined three consecutive quarters in FY 25/26. "Before Patient Interaction" rate went from 83% (Q1) to 70% (Q2) to 62% (Q3). "After Patient Interaction" went from 84% to 76% to 74%. This is the only quality metric trending in the wrong direction and is a flag worth watching.

**Sources**: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Hand-Hygiene-Compliance.pdf`, `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-safety.md` (Just Clean Your Hands program)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Numbers (FY 25/26)

| Moment | Q1 | Q2 | Q3 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Before Patient Interaction | 83% | 70% | **62%** |
| After Patient Interaction | 84% | 76% | 74% |

Both directions are downward each quarter. "Before Patient Interaction" is the weaker of the two categories.

(source: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Hand-Hygiene-Compliance.pdf`)

## What this measures

The "Just Clean Your Hands" program (Ministry of Health initiative) audits compliance with the World Health Organization's Five Moments of Hand Hygiene. The two publicly reported moments at GBGH are:

- **Before patient interaction**: cleaning before touching a patient or their immediate environment.
- **After patient interaction**: cleaning after touching a patient.

(See [Patient Safety](/patient-safety) for the broader IPAC program context.)

## Context for the decline

The PDF gives no explanation for the drop. Possible factors (not stated in the source):

- Survey methodology change.
- Staffing pressure or turnover.
- Audit visibility decline.
- Other operational stressors.

Treat the cause as an **open flag**.

## Strategic plan link

The PDF is branded with the "Continual Excellence" theme of the [Strategic Plan](/strategic-plan), which commits to "meeting or exceeding safety and quality standards." A sustained decline here is in tension with that direction.
