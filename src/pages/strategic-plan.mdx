# Strategic Plan 2023–2030

**Summary**: GBGH's seven-year strategic plan organized around four themes (Continual Excellence, Trust & Belonging, Dedicated Team, Sustainable Growth) and five core values (Inspire Dedication, Patients First, Empower Others, Lead with Empathy, Think Forward).

**Sources**: `pages/our-strategic-plan.md`, `pdfs/Strategic-Plan-Booklet-23-30-ENG-s.pdf` (not yet extracted)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Values

- **Inspire Dedication**: commitment to caring for patients, community, and each other.
- **Patients First**: listen, work as a team, collaborate with patients on safe and personalized care.
- **Empower Others**: foster belonging by investing in people and giving them space to grow.
- **Lead with Empathy**: lead with compassion, respect, and willingness to go the extra mile.
- **Think Forward**: pursue excellence through continuous improvement and innovation.

(source: `pages/our-strategic-plan.md`)

## Theme 1: Continual Excellence

Directions:

- Meet or exceed safety and quality standards.
- Provide person-centered and person-driven care.
- Pursue consistent and dependable care for all.
- Coordinate and collaborate with health partners for a more integrated care journey.

Objectives include evidence-informed leadership, continual collaboration with [Patient Family Advisory Council](/patient-family-advisory-council), and seamless transitions in care.

## Theme 2: Trust & Belonging

Directions:

- Deepen sense of belonging amongst people, patients, partners.
- Address health gaps and inequities in the community.
- Deepen relationships with Indigenous peoples (see [Land Acknowledgement](/land-acknowledgement) and [Indigenous Patient Navigator](/indigenous-patient-navigator)).
- Focus on Diversity, Equity, Inclusion, and Belonging.
- Instill confidence in healthcare services.

Objectives include trauma-informed care practices, active listening, partnerships, recognition of Francophone community needs (GBGH holds a partial French-language designation), and increased Indigenous representation in decision-making.

## Theme 3: Dedicated Team

Directions:

- Engaged, collaborative, inclusive team.
- Value, grow, invest in, retain talent.
- Continuous learning and growth.
- Strong internal culture, ingrained values.
- Celebrate successes; support performance.

Objectives include enhanced communication and recognition, investment in learning, cultivating collaboration, and inspiring a growth mindset.

## Theme 4: Sustainable Growth

Directions:

- Be an organization of choice for talented people.
- Grow services to align with community needs.
- Invest in physical infrastructure and environmental stewardship.
- Leverage technology to improve patient experience and outcomes.
- Enhance partnership networks.
- Achieve financial sustainability.

Specific growth focus areas named: women's health, mental health, seniors' health, surgical services, and diagnostic imaging.
