# Complex Continuing Care

**Summary**: Complex Continuing Care (CCC) is a transitional inpatient unit for medically complex patients who still need hospital-level care, regular on-site physician assessment, and active management by specialized staff. A province-wide scoring system determines eligibility. Average length of stay 90 days. CCC is not a final destination. Monthly co-payment fees apply.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-complex-continuing-care.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Purpose

CCC is for medically complex patients whose condition requires:

- Hospital stay.
- Regular on-site physician care and assessment.
- Active management by specialized staff.

It is **transitional**, not a final destination. Average length of stay: 90 days. The team helps the patient move toward improved functioning and personal independence, then helps arrange alternative living (home, retirement home, nursing home).

A province-wide scoring system is used to determine patient eligibility.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-complex-continuing-care.md`)

## Team

- Physicians, Nurse Practitioner (NP).
- Registered Nurse Team Leader, Registered Nurses (RN), Registered Practical Nurses (RPN), Personal Support Workers (PSW).
- Physical Therapists (PT), Occupational Therapists (OT), Speech-Language Pathologist (SLP), Physiotherapist Assistant (PTA).
- Dietitian (RD).
- Social Worker (SW).
- Other health professionals as needed.

## Patient and family responsibilities

- Active participation: set goals (e.g. stand, transfer, strength, endurance, toileting, device use), work with the team, link to community resources.
- **Monthly co-payment fees** are assessed for each patient.
- Purchase or rental of mobility aids and assistive devices as required (special wheelchairs, walkers).
- Goals reassessed regularly.
- Patient and family participate in interdisciplinary and family conferences.
- Family members invited to participate in care and therapy.

## Therapy schedule

- Individual and group sessions Mon-Fri, excluding holidays.
- Therapy provided based on patient needs, abilities, and resource availability.
