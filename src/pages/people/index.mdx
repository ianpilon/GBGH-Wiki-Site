## How to search

- **By name:** Obsidian quick switcher (Cmd+O), type any name. Each employee has their own note.
- **By role family:** see the hub pages below. Each lists its members.
- **By status:** filter on the `status` frontmatter field (`current` or `former`) using Obsidian search: `["status":"former"]`
- **Graph view:** open Obsidian graph view to see employees clustered by role family.
