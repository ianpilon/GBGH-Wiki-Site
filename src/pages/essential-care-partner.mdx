# Essential Care Partner Program

**Summary**: An Essential Care Partner (ECP) is a patient-designated companion who functions as part of the care team for vulnerable patients with admissions of 3+ days. Unlike a visitor (social role), an ECP supports the patient in essential ways: feeding, mobility, communication, supported decision-making. Up to two ECPs per patient. Available on 1 North, 2 North, 2 East, and ICU. Requires application, training (role + IPAC), badge, and unit-leader approval.

## What an ECP is

A patient-appointed person considered essential to their well-being while in hospital. Functions as part of the care team, not as a visitor. Can attend more frequently than regular visiting hours allow.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-essential-care-partner-program.md`)

## What ECPs do

- Support patients with cognitive, intellectual, or memory impairments.
- Support decision-making and care planning.
- Assist with eating, personal care, communication, or mobility.

## Eligibility

A patient is eligible for an ECP if:

- Admitted with an estimated length of stay of 3 days or longer, AND
- Meets the hospital's definition of a "vulnerable patient" (e.g. under 18, cognitive impairment, significant developmental/intellectual disability, frail elderly, mobility concerns, communication difficulties), AND
- Requires assistance with at least one of: personal care/feeding, mobility, communication (hearing/visual/speech/cognitive/intellectual/memory), supported decision-making.

Approval is by the inpatient unit leader.

## Units covered

- 1 North
- 2 North
- 2 East
- Intensive Care Unit

(Note: this list confirms 1 North as an inpatient unit, which is not listed on the [Medical Services](/medical-services) public page.)

## Limits

- Each eligible patient may designate up to **two ECPs**.
- ECPs function in their role only during the patient's admission.
- Only **one ECP at the bedside at a time**.

## Requirements of an ECP

Once approved, the ECP must:

- Complete ECP training (roles, responsibilities).
- Complete Infection Prevention and Control (IPAC) training.
- Adhere to GBGH safety protocols, Code of Conduct, Patient & Family Rights and Responsibilities.
- Adhere to IPAC standards.
- Be considerate and respectful of all patients, care team, staff, volunteers.
- Wear an ECP identification badge (issued by the Patient and Family Experience Office; returned at discharge).

## When ECPs can be present

During visiting hours. Must pass screening to enter and follow IPAC protocols.

## How to apply

- Patient or care team initiates.
- Complete application: `pdfs/GBGH-Essential-Care-Partner-Application-ENG.pdf`.
- Send to `patientrelations@gbgh.on.ca` (or ask care team for help).
- Allow 1 business day for the Patient & Family Experience Office to respond.

ECPs are not always required and not every patient qualifies.
