# Hospital Standardized Mortality Ratio (HSMR)

**Summary**: HSMR is a CIHI-calculated ratio comparing observed in-hospital deaths to expected deaths for a hospital's case mix. 100 = expected; &lt;100 = better than expected. GBGH's HSMR has dropped significantly across FY 25/26: 102 (Q1) → 71 (Q2) → 62 (Q3). The PDF notes 2025-26 cases are still open and subject to change.

**Sources**: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Hospital-Standardized-Mortality-Rate-HSMR.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Numbers (FY 25/26)

| Quarter | HSMR |
|---|---|
| Q1 | 102 |
| Q2 | 71 |
| Q3 | **62** |

(source: `pdfs/Q3-2025-26-Hospital-Standardized-Mortality-Rate-HSMR.pdf`)

## Interpretation

- **100 = expected**: deaths are at the rate expected for the hospital's case mix.
- **Below 100 = better than expected**.
- **Above 100 = worse than expected**.

GBGH started FY 25/26 slightly worse than expected (102) and ended Q3 substantially better than expected (62). The PDF caveats that all 2025-26 cases are considered open and subject to change until closed, so the Q1-Q3 figures may shift when finalized.

## Methodology source

HSMR is calculated by the Canadian Institute for Health Information (CIHI). It is one of the metrics GBGH shares with provincial agencies under [Health Records And Privacy](/health-records-and-privacy).
