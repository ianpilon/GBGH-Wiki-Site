# Careers at GBGH

**Summary**: GBGH is a 113-bed community hospital serving Midland, Penetanguishene, Christian Island, and the townships of Tiny, Tay, Georgian Bay, and Springwater. Population base ~55,000, swelling to 200,000+ for six summer months. Recruitment offers competitive salary, HOOPP pension, GreenShield Canada benefits, EFAP, mentorship, and a $25,000 out-of-province nurse incentive. Affiliations with NOSM, Rural Ontario Medical Program (ROMP), University of Toronto Temerty Faculty of Medicine.

**Sources**: `pages/join-our-teams.md`, `pages/join-our-teams-careers-at-gbgh.md`, `pages/join-our-teams-benefits.md`, `pages/join-our-teams-physician-recruitment.md`, `pages/join-our-teams-out-of-province-nurse-recruitment.md`, news posts on Temerty partnership and ROMP weeks

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Hospital and catchment

- 113-bed community hospital (per physician recruitment page).
- Serves Midland, Penetanguishene, Christian Island, Tiny, Tay, Georgian Bay, Springwater Townships.
- Year-round population base: ~55,000.
- Summer population: 200,000+ (six months of the year).
- 90 minutes north of Toronto.

(source: `pages/join-our-teams-physician-recruitment.md`)

## Applying

- Internal opportunities portal: `gbghemployees.talentpoolbuilder.com`.
- External opportunities portal: `gbgh.talentpoolbuilder.com`.

## Recruitment streams

### General staff (RNs, RPNs, allied health, support)

Standard external opportunities portal. Recruitment is supported by:

- HR coordination of [Housing For Healthcare](/housing-for-healthcare) referrals when housing is a deciding factor.

### Physician recruitment

- Chief of Staff: Dr. Vikram Ralhan (Site Liaison Clinician for NOSM, Assistant Professor at McMaster).
- Inquiries: `physicianrecruitment@gbgh.on.ca`.
- Coordinated through the **Georgian Bay Physician Recruitment** website: `georgianbayphysicianrecruitment.com/available-positions`.

### Out-of-province nurse recruitment

- $25,000 incentive for eligible out-of-province nurses.
- Help with cross-province licensing.
- Mentorship program for new graduates.
- Listing page: `gbgh.on.ca/join-our-teams/out-of-province-nurse-recruitment/`.

## Employee benefits

### Health and wellness

- Extended health, dental, and vision via **GreenShield Canada** (full-time).
- Employee and Family Assistance Program (EFAP) via **ComPsych**: 24/7 confidential counselling, stress, anxiety, family, financial, legal, grief, work-life balance.
- Wellness Committee.
- Wellness programs and events: Winter Market, Department Show and Tells, Wellness Fair, pumpkin carving, photo contests.
- Pet Therapy Policy (approved therapy animals; see [Pet Therapy](/pet-therapy)).

### Work-life balance

- Flexible scheduling.
- Paid time off (vacation and personal leave for full-time; percentage in lieu for part-time and casual).
- Pet bereavement support.

### Professional development

- Mentorship programs.
- Continuing education and training.
- Internal career-advancement pathways.
- Simulation lab.
- Professional Practice Model.
- Job shadowing.
- **GBGH Staff, Student, and Family Bursary**: annual financial support for an employee or their family member pursuing post-secondary education.

### Pension and financial

- **HOOPP** (Healthcare of Ontario Pension Plan): full-time and eligible part-time/casual.
- Wellness Loan and Interest-Free Education Loan.
- Disability and life insurance for full-time; optional group-rate life insurance.
- Part-time/casual receive percentage in lieu of health and insurance benefits.

### Other perks

- "GBGH Crew" recognition programs.
- Staff discounts at participating businesses.

(source: `pages/join-our-teams-benefits.md`)

## Academic affiliations

Per the physician recruitment page and news posts:

- **NOSM** (Northern Ontario School of Medicine): site liaison clinician role; community clerkships (most recent: 8-month clerkship announced September 2025).
- **Rural Ontario Medical Program (ROMP)**: hosts ROMP Weeks for new medical students; 2024 and 2025 cohorts mentioned in news posts.
- **University of Toronto, Temerty Faculty of Medicine**: partnership announced June 2024; family medicine residents welcomed June 2025.
- **Queen's University**: medical students welcomed for ROMP Week 2025.
- **McMaster University**: through the Chief of Staff's adjunct role.

## Values referenced in recruitment

The physician recruitment page lists hospital values as **Respect, Accountability, Excellence, and Compassion**.

Note: this differs from the five values on the public [Strategic Plan](/strategic-plan) page (Inspire Dedication, Patients First, Empower Others, Lead with Empathy, Think Forward). The "Respect, Accountability, Excellence, Compassion" set appears to be from a prior strategic plan period. **Treat as an open flag**: which values set is current? The 2023-2030 plan is the more recent document.
