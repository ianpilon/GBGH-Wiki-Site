## What the lab does

Staffed 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. Provides access to hundreds of tests daily across:

- Hematology.
- Chemistry.
- Coagulation.
- Blood transfusion medicine.
- Microbiology.

## Location and contact

- Level 1, Midland site.

## Patient experience

- Pre-appointment: follow physician's instructions on the requisition. Some tests need fasting.
- During: MLT selects a vein, uses sterile technique, draws blood into the required tubes.
- After: free to leave; notify the technician if feeling ill before leaving.
