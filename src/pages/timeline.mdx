# Timeline

**Summary**: Curated chronological timeline of significant GBGH events drawn from the news corpus and operational pages. Excludes routine annual events (flu shots, recurring awards) and unit COVID outbreaks (consolidated in [Covid 19 Response](/covid-19-response)). MRI saga has its own page: [Mri Project History](/mri-project-history). Dates marked "(lastmod)" come from each post's lastmod and may approximate publish date.

**Sources**: Cross-cutting from `raw/pages/`, [History](/history), [Covid 19 Response](/covid-19-response), [Mri Project History](/mri-project-history)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25
