# X-Ray

**Summary**: General X-ray (radiography) is the most common imaging exam. GBGH offers walk-in outpatient X-rays Mon-Fri 8am-4pm and added online booking in early 2025. A Medical Radiation Technologist is on-call 24/7 for ED and inpatients. A physician's requisition is mandatory; online booking without a requisition cannot be honoured. Most exams take 5-20 minutes.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-x-ray-services.md`, news posts "GBGH x-ray rooms undergo major renovation to better serve the community", "New Online Booking Now Available for X-Ray Appointments"

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## What X-ray is

A form of ionizing radiation used as a diagnostic tool. The image is formed when a detector senses X-rays passing through the body part being imaged. The most common imaging exam.

## Access and hours

- Walk-in outpatient X-rays Mon-Fri 8am-4pm; no appointment necessary.
- Trained Medical Radiation Technologist is available 24/7 for ED and inpatient X-rays.
- Online booking added (relatively recent news post): outlook.office365.com/book/XRaybookings@gbgh.on.ca/
- A completed physician's requisition is required. Online bookings without a requisition cannot be performed.
- Emergency patients take precedence; expect waits during busy periods.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-x-ray-services.md`)

## Location and contact

- Main level at the Midland site, near the Emergency Department.
- DI Reception: 705-526-1300 ext. 5090.

## Duration

- Most exams: ~15 minutes; range 5-20 minutes.

## During and after

- MRT positions the patient and asks them to hold still while the image is taken.
- Results from the Radiologist sent to ordering physician.
