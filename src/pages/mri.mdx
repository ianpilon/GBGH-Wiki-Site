# MRI

**Summary**: Magnetic Resonance Imaging is the newest modality in GBGH's Diagnostic Imaging department. The hospital opened its first MRI in 2025 after a multi-year fundraising and construction project. Most scans take 20-30 minutes (some up to one hour). The hospital does not provide sedation; patients with claustrophobia should arrange sedation with their own physician.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-magnetic-resonance-imaging-mri.md`, multiple news posts (MRI funding, breaking ground, delivery milestone, official opening)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What MRI is

A scan that uses a powerful magnet, radiofrequency waves, and a computer to create cross-sectional images of internal organs and structures. No ionizing radiation (unlike X-ray and CT). Scanner is an open-ended tube.

## Where

Diagnostic Imaging Department, main level at the Midland site, just past the switchboard, near the Emergency Department.

## Booking and duration

- Most scans: 20-30 minutes.
- Some scans: up to one hour.
- Emergency patients take precedence and may cause delays.
- DI Reception: 705-526-1300 ext. 5090.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-magnetic-resonance-imaging-mri.md`)

## Before the scan

- Must be free of metal: jewelry, piercings; change into hospital gown.
- MRI Technologist verbally reviews surgical history. If patient cannot do this, a family member must.
- **Implants**: critical to disclose early. Includes aneurysm clips/coils, stents, neurostimulators, orthopedic implants (hip/knee), cochlear implants. Most can be safely scanned but require research. A few cannot be scanned at GBGH. Bring implant card if available.
- **Glucose monitors**: some current models can stay on; older models must be removed.
- **Sedation**: NOT provided at GBGH. Patients with claustrophobia must arrange sedation through their own physician and bring a driver.
- **Pregnancy**: call the MRI department to discuss. Most MRIs are safe in pregnancy but some concerns must be addressed first.

## Scan preparation by type

- Standard with contrast: may require nothing to eat or drink for 4 hours before; possible IV for dye (MRI dye is different from CT dye; CT allergy does not necessarily mean MRI allergy).
- **MRI Enterography**: nothing to eat/drink for 4 hours; bring 2 litres of chilled apple or cranberry juice (do not drink before arrival); arrive 2 hours early; expect to be at the hospital 2.5-3 hours.
- **MRI Prostate**: avoid solid foods and dairy for 8 hours before; clear fluids only.

## During the scan

- Earplugs and headphones provided (loud knocking/clicking sounds).
- Hold still; may be asked to hold breath for a few seconds at a time.
- Two-way intercom with technologist.
- Squeeze button to alert technologist.

## After the scan

- If dye was used: remain in department for 15 minutes after injection.
- Results sent to ordering physician.
