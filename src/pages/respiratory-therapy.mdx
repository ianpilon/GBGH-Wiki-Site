# Respiratory Therapy

**Summary**: Registered Respiratory Therapists (RRTs) help patients with breathing problems. Therapies include oxygen delivery, nebulized medication (ventolin, atrovent, steroids, epinephrine), asthma management, and ventilator-assisted breathing for severe respiratory distress. Since April 2023, GBGH offers full pulmonary function testing (PFT) locally, eliminating the need for patients to leave the area for this assessment.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-respiratory-therapy.md`, news post "GBGH adding full pulmonary function testing to enhance access for local patients"

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What RRTs do

Help patients with breathing problems through:

- Oxygen delivery via mask.
- Medication delivery: ventolin, atrovent, steroids, epinephrine.
- Asthma management (assessment, prescribed medication, repeat treatments over hours as needed).
- Ventilator-assisted breathing for severe respiratory distress.
- Teaching patients and families about breathing problems, daily management of chronic disorders, and proper use of medication devices.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-respiratory-therapy.md`)

## Access

- **Outpatient tests**: register at Switchboard via main entrance; RRT comes to lobby for pickup.
- **Inpatients**: bedside care from RRT.

## Pulmonary Function Testing (PFT) since April 2023

As of April 2023, GBGH offers full PFT locally as part of growing services to community needs. Before this, patients had to leave the area for the test.

PFT is used to:

- Diagnose lung diseases (COPD, asthma, bronchitis, shortness of breath, emphysema).
- Monitor response to treatment.
- Guide further treatment and intervention decisions.
- Evaluate patients before elective surgery for fitness for general anesthetic.
- Assess medication effects and disease-treatment progress.

Patients are typically referred to PFT after basic spirometry shows decreased air flow. Referrals come through the primary care provider.
