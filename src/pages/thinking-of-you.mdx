# Thinking of You

**Summary**: An email service where loved ones can send messages to inpatients. Address: ThinkingOfYou@gbgh.on.ca. Messages are printed and delivered to patients on business days at 9am; emails received after that time are delivered the next business day.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-thinking-of-you.md`, news posts ("GBGH delivers messages to patients as part of 'Thinking of You' program")

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## How it works

- Send an email to `ThinkingOfYou@gbgh.on.ca` with the patient's name and your message.
- Messages are printed and delivered weekday mornings at 9am.
- Anything received after 9am goes out the following business day.

The web form on the page collects: sender name, sender email, patient name, message.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-thinking-of-you.md`)

## Origin

The program is referenced in older news posts, originally launched during the pandemic-era visitor restrictions. It continues today even with 24/7 family presence restored (see [Visiting Hours](/visiting-hours)).
