# my health care (Patient Portal)

**Summary**: A shared patient portal at myhealthcareportal.ca that gives patients and authorized friends, family members, and caregivers online access to their electronic health record from GBGH and partner hospitals. View appointments, pre-register, see visit history, review results, access dictated reports, update personal info. iOS and Android apps available. Note: documents must be printed from a web browser on Windows or macOS (not the mobile app).

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-my-healthcare.md`, news post "Local hospitals launch new patient portal", `pdfs/my-health-care-FAQs-public-May-2023-ENG.pdf`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it is

A secure online patient portal that shares records across partner hospitals. The vendor appears to be MEDITECH (the page mentions the "MEDITECH MHealth app" for printing limitations).

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-my-healthcare.md`)

## What patients can do

- View upcoming appointments.
- Pre-register for upcoming appointments.
- See visit history.
- Review results.
- Access dictated reports.
- Update personal information.

## Access

- Web: https://myhealthcareportal.ca/
- iOS and Android apps available in their respective stores.

## Authorized representatives

Friends, family, and caregivers can be authorized to view a patient's record.

## Limitations

- Documents cannot be printed from the MHealth mobile app or a mobile-browser session.
- Printing requires a web browser on Windows or macOS.
