# Thank Your Care Team

**Summary**: A web form for patients and families to send compliments and thanks to specific staff members or the GBGH team as a whole. Messages get routed to the named person or team.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-thank-your-care-team.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## How it works

The page hosts a simple web form (name, email, message). Submissions are delivered to the named person or team.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-thank-your-care-team.md`)

This is a complement to [Patient Relations](/patient-relations) (which handles concerns and complaints).
