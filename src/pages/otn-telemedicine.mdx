## What OTN is

The Ontario Telemedicine Network is an interactive, two-way televideo system that connects 1,200+ sites across Ontario, allowing face-to-face consults with providers who may be hundreds of kilometres away. Aims to reduce travel, time off work, and out-of-area trips for specialist visits.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-otn-ontario-telemedicine-network.md`)

## Location and hours

- Studio in [Ambulatory Care](/ambulatory-care) on the lower level.
- Hours: Monday-Friday, 8am-4pm.

## How to access

- Ask family physician about a telemedicine referral.
- OTN contacts patient directly with appointment date and time.
- Appointments take place in a private room; information handled under strict privacy guidelines.
