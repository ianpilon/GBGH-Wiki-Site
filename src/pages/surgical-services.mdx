# Surgical Services

**Summary**: GBGH's surgical program covers obstetrical (cesarean sections), gynecology, general surgery, orthopedic (day surgery only), dentistry, ENT, and ophthalmology. The OR runs booked surgeries Mon-Fri 7am-3pm and is on-call 24/7 for emergencies. Most surgeries are day surgery; some require short admission. Recent expansions include shoulder surgery, ERCP, urology, and a partnership with RVH on wait-times.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-surgical-services.md`, news posts on ERCP, shoulder surgery, urology expansion, and the RVH wait-times partnership

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Scope of procedures

- Obstetrical (cesarean sections, see [Obstetrical Unit](/obstetrical-unit)).
- Gynecology.
- General surgery (wide range).
- Orthopedic (day surgery only).
- Dentistry.
- ENT (ear, nose, throat).
- Ophthalmology.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-surgical-services.md`)

## Team

General surgeons, surgical specialists, dentists, anaesthetists, registered nurses, OR scheduler, OR support worker.

## Hours

- Booked surgeries: Monday-Friday, 7am-3pm.
- Available 24/7 for emergencies.

## Pre-op workflow

- Surgery booked, then Pre-Op Clinic appointment scheduled 2-3 weeks prior.
- At appointment, nurse covers food restrictions, medication instructions, blood work, x-rays.
- Patient is asked to complete the Anesthesia Questionnaire and review the Guide for Surgical Patients beforehand (PDF handouts).
- OR Scheduler: 705-526-1300 ext. 5247.

## Patient handouts

The hospital publishes ~30 procedure-specific patient handouts as PDFs. Examples:

- Adenoidectomy, Appendix Removal (Laparoscopic Appendectomy), Arthroscopy, Bowel Surgery (Patient and Caregiver Guide to Enhancing Recovery After Bowel Surgery), Breast Surgery, Cesarean Birth Post Partum, Cataract Surgery, Circumcision, Cystoscopy, Dental Extractions, D&C / Hysteroscopy, ERCP, Gallbladder Removal, Hand & Wrist, Hernia Repair, Laparoscopy, LAVH, LEEP, Myringotomy, Nasal Surgery, Orthopedic Hardware Removal, Rectal Surgery, Scrotal Surgery, Tonsillectomy, Total Laparoscopic Hysterectomy, TURBT, TURP, Tympanoplasty / Myringoplasty.

Full handout PDFs are in `raw/pdfs/`. A consolidated index will be built in Phase 4 (`patient-education-handouts`).

## Day-of and post-op logistics

- OR pickup parking is at designated spaces near the Cottage outbuilding.
- 30-minute grace period in the lot before parking fees apply.
- Pickup driver is contacted by the recovery nurse with an estimated pickup time.
- Sedated patients are brought to the main entrance by wheelchair; nurse assists into vehicle. Drivers must not leave vehicles unattended at the main entrance.
