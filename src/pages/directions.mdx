# Directions

**Summary**: GBGH's Midland site is on Highway 93 just north of Hugel Avenue, on the east side of the highway. From the south (Barrie): Highway 400 north to Highway 93. From the north (Parry Sound): Highway 400 south, then Highway 12 west to Midland. Landmarks before the hospital: Mountainview Mall, Canadian Tire.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-directions.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Address

1112 St. Andrew's Drive, Midland, Ontario.

## From the south (Barrie area)

Highway 400 north, then Highway 93 into Midland.

## From the north (Parry Sound area)

Highway 400 south, take Highway 12 west exit to Midland.

## Landmarks before the hospital

Watch for the Mountainview Mall and the Canadian Tire on Highway 93. The hospital entrance is on the east side of the highway, just north of the Hugel Avenue intersection.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-directions.md`)
