## What this measures

VRE refers to strains of Enterococcus bacteria resistant to vancomycin. "Hospital-acquired" or "nosocomial" cases are counted; community-acquired ones are excluded.

VRE is one of the three antibiotic-resistant bacteria named on the [Patient Safety](/patient-safety) page as IPAC priorities.
