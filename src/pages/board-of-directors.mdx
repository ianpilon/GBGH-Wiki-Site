# Board of Directors

**Summary**: GBGH is governed by a Board of Directors composed of community representatives, hospital staff, and two representatives from the medical staff. All board members serve as volunteers. As of 2025-2026 the Chair is Bernie Uhlich.

**Sources**: `pages/board-of-directors.md`, `pages/public-reporting.md`

## Recruitment

The board recruits community-service-minded individuals on a rolling basis. Recruitment priorities listed: diverse representation in experience (clinical, health system, advocacy) and background, including Indigenous and Francophone representation.

- Online application: a Microsoft Forms link is on the board page.
- Recruitment contact: `GBGHBoard@gbgh.on.ca`, 705-526-1300 x5016.
- General inquiries to the President & CEO's office: 705-526-5011.
