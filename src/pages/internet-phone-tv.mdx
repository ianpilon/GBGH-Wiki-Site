# Internet, Phone, and TV

**Summary**: Wi-Fi is free for patients and visitors. As of January 2024, GBGH switched to a bring-your-own-device model for entertainment and phone calls. TVs are no longer in patient rooms on 2 North and 2 East. Landline phones were removed from all patient rooms. If a patient lacks a phone or tablet, the hospital can lend one.

## Bring-your-own-device (since January 2024)

The hospital transitioned to a bring-your-own-device model. Effects:

- **TVs removed** from patient rooms on 2 North and 2 East inpatient units.
- **Landline phones removed** from all patient rooms.
- Patients use their own phones and tablets for entertainment and phone calls.
- Hospital can **lend an iPad or phone** to patients without a device. Ask the care team.

## Why this matters

Combined with the [What To Bring](/what-to-bring) page, this means:

- Patients should bring their own phones, tablets, chargers, headphones.
- Wi-Fi is the entertainment channel; no more bedside TVs or landline phones.
- For loaned hospital devices, ask the care team.
