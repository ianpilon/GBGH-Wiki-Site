# Accreditation and Awards

**Summary**: GBGH holds the highest level of recognition from Accreditation Canada, "Accredited with Exemplary Standing", awarded after a four-day survey in November 2023. The hospital met more than 99% of approximately 2,100 standards and 100% of the 30 Required Organizational Practices (ROPs). The previous survey in 2019 also resulted in Exemplary Standing (98% / 100%).

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## 2023 survey

- Dates: November 20-23, 2023.
- Surveyors: four healthcare experts from across Canada.
- Result: Accredited with Exemplary Standing.
- Standards met: more than 99% of approximately 2,100.
- Required Organizational Practices (ROPs): 100% of 30 achieved.

Strengths noted by surveyors:

- Dedication of staff and professional staff.
- Safety rounds and quality plans providing a standard approach across the hospital.
- Engagement with patients and family members in planning and decision-making.
- Relationships with community partners.
- Medication safety framework.
- Falls prevention program.

(source: `pages/accreditation-awards.md`)

## 2019 survey

- Result: Accredited with Exemplary Standing.
- Standards met: 98%.
- ROPs achieved: 100%.

## About Accreditation Canada

An independent, not-for-profit Canadian organization that evaluates more than 1,100 healthcare and social services organizations against best-practice standards. Accreditation is voluntary. Surveyors are peers with extensive healthcare experience.
