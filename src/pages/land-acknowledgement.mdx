# Land Acknowledgement

**Summary**: GBGH's official land acknowledgement names the Anishinabek people, the Coldwater Narrows Treaty, and the Williams Treaties Territories, and lists nine specific First Nations. The acknowledgement is paired with commitments around health-outcome gaps, anti-racism, and cultural safety.

**Sources**: `pages/gbgh-land-acknowledgement.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## The acknowledgement (full text)

Georgian Bay General Hospital acknowledges that the land on which it is located is the traditional territory of the Anishinabek people. We recognize our location rests within the ancestral lands of the Coldwater Narrows Treaty and Williams Treaties Territories. We acknowledge Alderville First Nation, Beausoleil First Nation, Curve Lake First Nation, Georgina Island First Nation, Hiawatha First Nation, Nawash First Nation, Rama First Nation, Scugog Island First Nation members, families past and present.

## Commitments stated in the acknowledgement

- Strive to unify the relationship between Indigenous and non-Indigenous people.
- Identify gaps in health service and establish measurable goals in health outcomes between Indigenous and non-Indigenous communities.
- Work towards equality, serving all patients with respect, compassion, understanding, and empathy without prejudice or racism.
- Embrace knowledge sharing to improve allyship in providing culturally safe healthcare to all First Nations, Métis, and Inuit patients.
