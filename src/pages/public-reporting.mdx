# Public Reporting

**Summary**: Index of GBGH's publicly reported documents. The hospital publishes accreditation results, quality improvement plans, quarterly safety and infection indicators, annual reports, audited financial statements, compliance attestations, accountability agreements with the Ministry of Health, accessibility plans, expense reporting, and a Bill S-211 forced labour attestation.
