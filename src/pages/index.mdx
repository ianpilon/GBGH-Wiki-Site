## Phase 2: Programs and services

### Acute and emergency

- [Emergency Department](/emergency-department) — 24/7 ED; ~120/day; alternatives the hospital promotes.
- [Intensive Care Unit](/intensive-care-unit) — Level 3; 6 critical-care beds + 10 telemetry beds.
- [Ed Wait Times Clock](/ed-wait-times-clock) — Live Oculys wait-times page; Rocket Doctor partnership.

### Surgery and ambulatory

- [Surgical Services](/surgical-services) — general / ortho / dental / ENT / ophth / obstetric / gyn; Mon-Fri OR; 35+ patient handouts.
- [Ambulatory Care](/ambulatory-care) — outpatient clinic on lower level; integrated team.

### Obstetrics

- [Obstetrical Unit](/obstetrical-unit) — Level I birthing; Joint Birthing Program with OSMH (Level II); Prenatal Clinic closed 2025-12-15.

### Diagnostic imaging

- [Diagnostic Imaging](/people/diagnostic-imaging) — parent page; lists all modalities and the echo satellite with RVH.
- [Mri](/mri) — first MRI at GBGH; operational 2025; sedation NOT provided on site.
- [Ct Scan](/ct-scan) — 15-min scans; prep varies by body part.
- [X Ray](/x-ray) — walk-in Mon-Fri 8-4; online booking added 2025.
- [Ultrasound](/ultrasound) — 30-min typical; prep varies (abdominal vs pelvic vs renal vs scrotum vs thyroid).
- [Mammography](/mammography) — Phase 1 screening from 2026-04-13; diagnostic from 2026-05-04; CAR-MAP accreditation.

### Clinical support

- [Clinical Laboratory](/clinical-laboratory) — 24/7 lab; near-perfect international accreditation score.
- [Pharmacy](/pharmacy) — inpatient only.
- [Respiratory Therapy](/respiratory-therapy) — includes full PFT since April 2023.
- [Rehabilitation](/rehabilitation) — inpatient rehab, multidisciplinary team.

### Inpatient and long-term

- [Medical Services](/medical-services) — 1 North + 2 North + 2 East; inpatient ECP coverage including ICU.
- [Complex Continuing Care](/complex-continuing-care) — transitional unit; ~90 day stays; co-payment fees apply.
- [Palliative Care](/palliative-care) — MAiD and palliative care launched 2023-08-08.
- [Dialysis](/dialysis) — 9-station hemodialysis at 611 Prospect Blvd; OSMH satellite.

### Outpatient and remote

- [Otn Telemedicine](/otn-telemedicine) — OTN studio in Ambulatory Care.
- [Gbgh At Home](/gbgh-at-home) — up to 16 weeks of post-discharge in-home support.
- [My Health Care](/my-health-care) — MEDITECH patient portal (myhealthcareportal.ca).

### Support services

- [Food Services](/food-services) — patient meals + cafeteria; Mon-Fri only for cafeteria.
- [Discharge Planning](/discharge-planning) — 10am discharge; cashier visit; out-of-country charges due at discharge.
