# COVID-19 Response

**Summary**: GBGH's COVID-19 response in the news corpus spans March 2020 through April 2024 (the last documented unit outbreak). Patterns: a Midland Assessment Centre operated at and near GBGH; visitation restrictions tightened and loosened repeatedly; mandatory visitor vaccination was lifted June 2022; passive screening began November 2022; the hospital returned to 24/7 family presence May 24, 2023 (see [Visiting Hours](/visiting-hours)). Unit-level COVID outbreaks were declared and resolved at least ~12 times across 1 North, 2 North, and 2 East.

**Sources**: 51 posts from the `covid` category of `raw/_index.json`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

## Unit outbreak timeline

GBGH publicly declared and resolved unit-level COVID outbreaks throughout the pandemic. Counts by unit (across the corpus):

| Unit | Outbreak declarations |
|---|---|
| 1 North | 8 declarations (Dec 2020 first, April 2024 last) |
| 2 East | 4 declarations |
| 2 North | 3 declarations |

The first major outbreak in the corpus was declared December 7, 2020. Three inpatient units were involved by mid-December 2020. The outbreak was declared over January 12, 2021.

The most recent COVID outbreak in the corpus: 1 North declared April 8, 2024, declared over April 22, 2024.

## Midland COVID-19 Assessment Centre

- Operated at GBGH initially.
- Online appointment booking added June 29, 2021.
- Relocated from GBGH site November 15, 2021.
- Late 2021 saw "unprecedented demand for testing" (post Dec 31, 2021).
- A "Community Assessment Centre available for expanded testing" post is in the corpus (lastmod 2023-11-15).
