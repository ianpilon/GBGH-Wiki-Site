## What this measures

The Surgical Safety Checklist is a 19-item checklist run at three points around every operation: before anesthesia ("sign in"), before skin incision ("time out"), and before patient leaves OR ("sign out"). Tracks whether the checklist was completed.

GBGH reports compliance both monthly and rolled to quarter. Quarterly views show 100% throughout.
