## What this measures

VAP is pneumonia that develops in a patient receiving mechanical ventilation, typically diagnosed 48+ hours after intubation. It is one of the standard ICU quality indicators.

GBGH's ICU is described as a Level 3 unit with 6 critical-care beds and the ability to support ventilator-dependent patients (see [Intensive Care Unit](/intensive-care-unit)). A consistent zero VAP rate is a notable clean record for the program.
