## What this measures

MRSA is an antibiotic-resistant strain of Staphylococcus aureus. "Hospital-acquired" means newly identified during the patient's stay (vs. brought in from the community). MRSA is named on the [Patient Safety](/patient-safety) page as one of the three antibiotic-resistant bacteria GBGH's IPAC program explicitly targets.
