# Indigenous Patient Navigator

**Summary**: The Indigenous Patient Navigator (Anishinaabek name: E-naadmaaged Enaapinedjig Anishinaabek) supports First Nations, Métis, and Inuit (FNMI) patients and families through the hospital and into community services with culturally appropriate care. Program runs in partnership with Beausoleil First Nation and the Barrie Area Native Advisory Circle's Aboriginal Health Circle. Access requires self-identification at registration and consent.

## Partnership

The program is delivered in partnership with:

- Beausoleil First Nation.
- The Barrie Area Native Advisory Circle's Aboriginal Health Circle.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-indigenous-patient-navigator.md`)

## Role of the Navigator

The Navigator supports FNMI patients and families to receive the best care possible at GBGH and in community services, with culturally appropriate care. Specific responsibilities:

- Support FNMI patients through triage and admissions.
- Communicate and liaise between patient, family, hospital, and care team.
- Help FNMI patients understand hospital services.
- Assist through a coordinated care plan.
- Increase primary-care involvement for the chronically ill via the North Simcoe Community Health Links.
- Help patients and families access FNMI and non-FNMI health support upon discharge.
- Advocate for the best care possible for FNMI patients.

## Cultural services

- Access to Healing and Smudge Room (Anishinaabek name: **Neegan Naabwag**).
- Smudging.
- Traditional medicines.
- Referral to traditional practitioner or healer.
- Referral to Indigenous community services.

## Traditional Healing and Smudge Room

- Located in the 2 North corridor junction toward 2 East rooms.
- To access: contact the Indigenous Patient Navigator at ext. 5356, or 705-529-1682, or Security at ext. 5401.

## How to access services

1. **Self-identify** as FNMI (status or non-status) at registration.
2. **Provide First Nation or Inuit status card number** at registration if applicable.
3. **Consent** is required to receive services.
   - Meet and greet (offer of services).
   - Acceptance optional.
   - Consent required for service delivery.
