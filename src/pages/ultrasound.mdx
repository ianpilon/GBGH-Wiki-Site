# Ultrasound

**Summary**: Ultrasound uses sound waves to produce real-time images of the inside of the body. Most exams take ~30 minutes (range 15-60). Preparation varies by exam (abdominal, obstetrical, pelvic, renal, scrotum, thyroid, musculoskeletal). A Family Presence policy specifically for ultrasound is published.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-ultrasound.md`, news posts on expansion ("GBGH expands ultrasound service to better serve the community", "Ultrasound wait times at all-time low at GBGH")

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What ultrasound is

Sound-wave imaging that produces real-time pictures of the inside of the body. Used to assess organs and vessels, monitor pregnancies, detect abnormalities, and assist with medical procedures. Images reviewed by a Radiologist.

## Where and duration

- Diagnostic Imaging, main level near the Emergency Department.
- Most exams: ~30 minutes; range 15-60 minutes.
- DI Reception: 705-526-1300 ext. 5090.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-diagnostic-imaging-ultrasound.md`)

## Preparation by exam type

| Exam | Preparation |
|---|---|
| Abdominal | Normal supper the day before. Nothing to eat/drink after 10pm. No breakfast morning of exam. |
| Obstetrical, pelvis, renal | Drink 4 cups (32 oz) of liquid (water, tea, coffee), finish one hour before. **Do not empty bladder.** |
| Scrotum | None required |
| Thyroid | None required |
| Musculoskeletal | None required |

## During the exam

- Lie on the examination bed.
- Technologist applies water-based gel.
- A probe is applied with varying pressure to produce black-and-white images.
- The Ultrasound Technologist cannot interpret images or give a verbal report.

## After

Results from the Radiologist are sent to the ordering physician.
