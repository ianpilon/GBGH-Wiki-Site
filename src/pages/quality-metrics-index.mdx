# Quality Metrics Index

**Summary**: GBGH publicly reports nine quality and safety indicators each quarter. Latest available are Q3 FY 25/26 for seven indicators, Q4 for SSC. As of Q3 FY 25/26: four hospital-acquired infection metrics (CLI, MRSA, VAP, VRE) are at **zero reported cases** for the full year-to-date. CDI fluctuated (2 → 4 → 2 cases). HSMR improved substantially (102 → 71 → 62). SSC compliance was effectively 100%. Hand hygiene compliance is **trending down**: Before-Patient went 83% → 70% → 62%.

## Reading guide

- All metrics use Q1 = Apr-Jun, Q2 = Jul-Sep, Q3 = Oct-Dec, Q4 = Jan-Mar (Ontario hospital fiscal year, Apr-Mar).
- **Hand Hygiene** and the four "clean" infection metrics are concrete percentages or case counts. **HSMR** is a ratio (100 = expected; &lt;100 better, >100 worse) computed by CIHI; GBGH notes that 2025-26 cases are still open and subject to change.
- The Strategic Plan's "Continual Excellence" theme covers all these (each PDF is branded with the theme).
- Older reporting periods exist on the [Public Reporting](/public-reporting) page but are not yet ingested here.

## Notable

- **Strong:** four hospital-acquired infection categories (CLI, MRSA, VAP, VRE) at zero cases for nine months running.
- **Improving:** HSMR has dropped from 102 (worse than expected) to 62 (substantially better than expected).
- **Concern:** Hand hygiene compliance has fallen in both "Before Patient" and "After Patient" categories each quarter, with "Before Patient" dropping to 62%. This is the only metric trending in the wrong direction. Worth watching as a quality flag.
