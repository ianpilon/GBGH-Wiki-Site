# Health Records and Privacy

**Summary**: Consolidates three GBGH pages: Health Records, Privacy Statement, and FIPPA. Personal health information is governed by Ontario's PHIPA (2004); other GBGH records are subject to FIPPA (since Jan 1, 2012). The Regional Privacy Office handles privacy across GBGH and RVH. A new development as of 2025: GBGH physicians may use an Ambient AI Scribe (Microsoft Nuance DAX Copilot, via RVH) to take notes during visits; patient consent is required.

## Two different laws govern two different categories

| Type of information | Governing law | How to request |
|---|---|---|
| Personal health information (PHI): your hospital record | **PHIPA** (Personal Health Information Protection Act, 2004) | Release of Information Form (`pdfs/GBGH-Release-of-Information-Form-Nov-2025-ENG.pdf`); fax 705-526-4491 |
| Other personal information held by the hospital | **FIPPA** (Freedom of Information and Protection of Privacy Act, since 2012-01-01) | Access Request Form + $5 application fee |

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-health-records.md`, `pages/patient-and-visitor-privacy-statement.md`, `pages/patient-and-visitor-freedom-of-information-and-protection-of-privacy-act.md`)

## Accessing your own health record

- Right of access is provided by law.
- No charge to send copies to another physician or healthcare provider under the circle of care.
- Adult patients consent themselves if capable.
- If not capable, consent comes from a substitute decision maker in order of priority: guardian of person or property; attorney for personal care or property; representative appointed by the Consent and Capacity Board; spouse or partner; child or parent; parent with right of access only; sibling; other relative.

## Accessing a deceased patient's record

Need either:

- A copy of the will naming you as executor, or
- A notarized letter from a lawyer confirming legal signing authority.

The hospital does not issue death certificates. A Medical Certificate of Death goes to the funeral home. The Office of the Registrar General (1-800-461-2156) issues the Death Certificate.

## Errors in your record

Use the Request for Corrections Form (`pdfs/Request-for-Correction-to-PHI-and-PI-Form-Regional.pdf`). For more info, contact the Regional Privacy Office.

## What the hospital does with your information

Under PHIPA, the hospital uses your information to:

- Treat and care for you.
- Maintain a record of your care.
- Obtain payment (OHIP, WSIB).
- Plan, administer, and manage internal operations.
- Conduct risk management and quality improvement.
- Improve quality of services (post-care surveys).
- Compile statistics.
- Support research and education.
- For fundraising (name and address only).
- Coordinate your care with other providers (incl. shared electronic systems).
- Comply with legal/regulatory requirements.

## Whom your information may be disclosed to

Listed external recipients include:

- External electronic health record systems: CIMS, ISCIS, IAR, eCHN.
- Ministry of Health/LTC e-health: EMPI, WTIS, Drug Profile Viewer, ORRS, OLIS, ConnectingOntario.
- Provincial agencies: Cancer Care Ontario, ICES (Institute for Clinical Evaluative Sciences), CIHI, OICR, BORN, Trillium Gift of Life.
- Researchers (with REB approval).
- Medical Officer of Health (communicable disease reporting).
- Workplace Safety & Insurance Board.
- Law enforcement (with warrant or subpoena, or to aid investigation).
- Children's Aid Society.
- Public Guardian and Trustee.
- Coroner's Office.

## Your choice (lockbox)

You can withdraw or withhold consent for some uses or disclosures via the Lockbox Fact Sheet and Request Form (`pdfs/GBGH-Lockbox-Fact-Sheet-Form-Download.pdf`). Subject to exceptions where disclosure is required by law.

Unless told otherwise, GBGH may:

- Tell visitors and callers that you are a patient and which unit you are on.
- Disclose PHI to external healthcare providers for your care.
- Share information with provincial electronic databases.
- Provide your name, location, and religious affiliation (only if you stated one) to spiritual support representatives.
- Share information for Quality Improvement (e.g. NRC Health surveys).
- Disclose name and address to the GBGH Foundation for fundraising.

## Ambient AI Scribe (in trial as of 2025)

GBGH physicians **may opt** to use an Ambient AI Scribe (Microsoft Nuance DAX Copilot, via RVH). The tool listens during the visit, generates a draft note, the physician reviews and edits, then it goes in your record.

- Temporary audio recordings are deleted once notes are finalized.
- Only your physician and care team can access the information.
- Patient information stored outside of Canada is de-identified and may be used for system learning and refinement.
- **Patient consent is required**. You can decline or change your mind at any time, with no effect on the care you receive.
- More information: `https://www.rvh.on.ca/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/RVH-and-Microsoft-Nuance-DAX-Copilot.pdf` (in `raw/pdfs/`).
- Questions: ask your physician or contact the Regional Privacy Office (`regionalprivacy@rvh.on.ca`).

This is a fairly new disclosure on a hospital privacy page and worth flagging because it is the only mention of AI in patient-facing GBGH content.

## FIPPA process and fees

- Application fee: $5 (non-refundable) payable to GBGH.
- Search/preparation: $7.50 per quarter hour for general records.
- Photocopying: $0.20 per page.
- CDs/storage media: $10.
- You are advised if fees will exceed $25; deposit of 50% requested if cost exceeds $100.
- Appeals go to the Information and Privacy Commissioner of Ontario.

Personal health information is NOT accessible via FIPPA — it is subject to PHIPA (use the health-records request process above).
