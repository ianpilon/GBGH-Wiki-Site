# GBGH@home

**Summary**: A post-discharge in-home support program lasting up to 16 weeks. Team includes a navigator, nurses, PSWs, OT, PT, SLP, dietitian, social worker. First visit scheduled before discharge. Daily check-ins for the first week, then frequency adjusted. After 16 weeks, patients are transitioned to Home and Community Care Support Services (Ontario Health).

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-gbghhome.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## What it provides

Care and support at home after discharge from GBGH. Team:

- Navigator.
- Nurses.
- Personal Support Workers (PSWs).
- Occupational Therapists (OT).
- Physiotherapists (PT).
- Dietitians.
- Speech-Language Pathologists (SLP).
- Social Workers.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-gbghhome.md`)

## Onboarding

Before discharge: team meets with patient, family, hospital team, GBGH@home team, and home-care provider to create a "first 72 hours" care plan. First home visit scheduled before discharge.

## What happens at home

- Day of discharge: phone call from GBGH@home to confirm safe arrival.
- First home visit within 24 hours of return home.
- Daily check-ins for the first week.
- After week one: patient and team decide frequency.
- Ongoing care-plan completion; updates to family doctor/nurse practitioner.
- Check-ins via home visits, phone, or video.
- Connections to community resources (Meals on Wheels, transportation, caregiver support).
- 24/7 phone number for questions/concerns.

## Duration and transitions

- Program lasts up to 16 weeks.
- If readmitted: GBGH@home continues support and helps with the return-home transition.
- Around 8 weeks: progress reviewed; ongoing-care plan begun.
- Around 12 weeks: if ongoing care is needed, team connects patient to a Home and Community Care Support Services care coordinator (Ontario Health) for assessment and a long-term plan.
