# Pharmaceutical Services

**Summary**: The hospital pharmacy supports inpatients only. It does not dispense medications to ED or outpatient patients. Staffed by pharmacists and pharmacy technicians; provides drug distribution, drug information, patient medication-profile monitoring, drug-use evaluation, parenteral nutrition, IV batching, and adverse drug reaction monitoring.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-pharmacy.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Scope

The hospital pharmacy is for inpatient medications. It is not a public-facing pharmacy. The Emergency Department page explicitly states that the hospital does not dispense drugs for ED patients; ED staff direct patients to local community pharmacies on request.

## Services

- Drug distribution.
- Drug information.
- Patient medication profile monitoring.
- Drug use evaluation.
- Parenteral nutrition.
- IV batching.
- Adverse drug reaction monitoring.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-pharmacy.md`)

## Team

Combination of pharmacists and pharmacy technicians, working as part of the broader inpatient healthcare team.

## Note

The hospital page also embeds a "Medication Safety" video (by Dr. Mike Evans, ISMP Canada) with patient-facing resources (safemedicationuse.ca, ismp-canada.org). Those are general patient-education links, not GBGH-specific.
