# Patient & Family Advisory Council (PFAC)

**Summary**: PFAC provides patient and family perspective to GBGH's decision-making. The council is volunteer-based and recruits members willing to share their GBGH stories and feedback. PFAC is explicitly cited as a partner in the 2023-2030 Strategic Plan's Continual Excellence theme. Application form is a PDF; contact PatientRelations at 705-526-1300 ext. 5022.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-family-advisory-council-pfac.md`, `pdfs/11-PFAC-Application-Form-July-2021.pdf` (form, not extracted)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Why PFAC matters

The hospital states that patient and family perspectives are essential to its decision-making. PFAC members share their experiences and, by thinking beyond personal experience, provide structured feedback on how the hospital can improve.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-patient-family-advisory-council-pfac.md`)

## Strategic context

PFAC is named in the [Strategic Plan](/strategic-plan) under Theme 1 (Continual Excellence) as an active partner: "Continually collaborate with and engage our Patient & Family Advisory Council (PFAC) to ensure patient experiences are incorporated into decision-making."
