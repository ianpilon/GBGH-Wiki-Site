# Dialysis Services

**Summary**: GBGH operates a 9-station hemodialysis unit at 611 Prospect Boulevard, Midland. It is a satellite of the Orillia Soldiers' Memorial Hospital (OSMH) regional dialysis program. The site is for patients receiving hemodialysis who live in North Simcoe. A news post in the corpus marks the opening of this Midland location ("GBGH officially opens new dialysis site in Midland").

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-dialysis-services.md`, `pages/programs-and-services-ambulatory-care.md`, news post on dialysis site opening

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Site

- **Location**: 611 Prospect Boulevard, Midland.
- **Stations**: 9.
- **Modality**: hemodialysis.
- **Catchment**: patients in North Simcoe.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-dialysis-services.md`)

## Partnership

The site is a satellite of OSMH's regional dialysis program. Kidney care resources are published by OSMH at osmh.on.ca/kidney-care-resources/.
