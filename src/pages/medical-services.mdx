# Medical Services (Acute Care Inpatient)

**Summary**: GBGH's acute care inpatient units are on the main floor at the Midland site: 2 East (medical) and 2 North (surgical and obstetrics). 69 beds total. Care is provided by physicians, RNs, RPNs, palliative care, diabetes resource, and unit support workers, with pharmacy, respiratory therapy, dietary, IT, and housekeeping support.

**Sources**: `pages/programs-and-services-medical-services.md`, references to "2 East" and "2 North" in news posts about outbreaks (COVID, C. difficile, enteric)

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Layout

| Unit | Use |
|---|---|
| 2 East | Medical |
| 2 North | Surgical and Obstetrics |

Both on the main floor, just off the lobby at the Midland site. Combined ~69 acute-care beds.

(source: `pages/programs-and-services-medical-services.md`)

## Care providers

- Physicians.
- Registered nurses (RNs) and registered practical nurses (RPNs).
- Palliative care team (see [Palliative Care](/palliative-care)).
- Diabetes resource.
- Unit support workers.

Supported by [Pharmacy](/pharmacy), [Respiratory Therapy](/respiratory-therapy), dietary, information systems, and housekeeping.

## 1 North is also an inpatient unit (not on the public page)

The Medical Services page lists only 2 East and 2 North, but the [Essential Care Partner](/essential-care-partner) page confirms 1 North is also a covered inpatient unit (its ECP coverage area is "1 North, 2 North, 2 East, ICU"). COVID outbreak news posts repeatedly reference 1 North too. The public-facing Medical Services page appears to be out of date or selectively focused on the surgical/OBS-adjacent units. Treat the **full inpatient inventory as: 1 North, 2 North, 2 East, plus the ICU**.
