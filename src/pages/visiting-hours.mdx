# Visiting Hours (Family Presence)

**Summary**: GBGH returned to 24/7 family presence on May 24, 2023. Visitors may come at any time based on the patient's preference. Late-night arrivals must enter through the ED for escort to the unit. Quiet times are 10pm-6am daily. Masking is required throughout the visit. The number of visitors at the bedside is decided by patient, family, and care team.

**Sources**: `pages/patient-and-visitor-visiting-hours.md`

**Last updated**: 2026-05-25

---

## Policy

Family presence is welcome 24 hours a day, based on patient preference. The hospital's framing: "Nobody knows a patient better than their loved ones."

Effective May 24, 2023, GBGH returned to 24/7 family presence after pandemic-era restrictions.

(source: `pages/patient-and-visitor-visiting-hours.md`)

## Late-night arrivals (ED entrance required)

If visiting during these hours, you must enter through the Emergency Department:

| Day | Hours |
|---|---|
| Monday–Friday | 8pm-6:30am |
| Saturday & Sunday | 8pm-8am |

The ED registration clerk takes your name, notifies the unit, and arranges escort.

## Quiet times

Quiet hours are 10pm-6am every day to promote a restful environment.

## Bedside visitor count

Decided collaboratively by the patient, family, and care team. Patients with Essential Care Partners have a different model; see [Essential Care Partner](/essential-care-partner) (only one ECP at the bedside at a time).

## Visitor requirements (still in force)

- Self-screen for symptoms per posted signage.
- Wear a hospital-provided mask upon entering.
- Stay masked for the visit.
- Do not visit if you or family members have colds, flu symptoms, or a known communicable disease.

## Allergy alerts

- No latex balloons (foil only).
- No lilies or strongly scented flowers.
- GBGH is fragrance-friendly: discourage perfume and cologne.

See [What To Bring](/what-to-bring) for the full allergy and scent policy.
